# Academic & Research Library Redesign Architecture & Prototype

## Overview
To elevate the digital library from a media/consumer streaming shelf into an **authoritative Academic & Research Library**, an interactive prototype was generated in [`.php_tmp/academic-library-index.php`](file:///c:/Users/Heste/OneDrive/Documents/_legacy_backup/.php_tmp/academic-library-index.php).

## Architectural Pillars Demonstrated in the Prototype

### 1. Institutional Archival Gateway
- **Header & Holdings Meta**: Displays institutional scope (`120+ Curated Volumes`, `9 Subject Research Desks`, `Open Access OER`, `Public Domain`).
- **Unified Academic Discovery Bar**: Real-time search with field selectors (`All Fields`, `Title`, `Author`, `Subject / FAST`, `Call Number LCC/DDC`).
- **Quick Archive Filters**: Instant sorting by `Primary Sources (1776–1945)`, `Mathematics & Proofs`, `Classical Literature`, and `Constitutional Law`.

### 2. Specialized Reading Rooms & Research Desks Gateway
- Direct showcase of the 9 Subject Research Desks as virtual **"Research Reading Rooms"** with holdings counters and direct deep-linking `#` hashed codes (`#cd90fec6`, `#6b3a0fb1`, `#2a870513`, etc.).

### 3. Scholar Research Carrel & Working Portfolio
- Institutional workspace replacing casual gamification with:
  - **Study Notebook Integration**: Marginalia, highlights, and flashcard exports.
  - **Bibliographic Card Catalog Registry**: Direct toggle to high-density tabular view.
  - **Sourcing & OER Licensing**: Direct access to Fair Use and Project Gutenberg statements.

### 4. Dual Catalog Browsing Modes
- **Dossier Card Stacks**: Rich cards with prominent Call Number ribbons (`LCC`, `DDC`), Lexile measures, and publication dates.
- **Card Catalog Table (Bibliographic Registry)**: High-density academic table displaying `Call Number | Title & Author | Discipline | Date | Lexile | Archival Actions (Read / Quick Cite)`.
