## Accessibility & Inclusion

We believe learning should be accessible to everyone. We are currently implementing native support for the **OpenDyslexic** typeface across all our modules.

- **Weighted Bottoms:** OpenDyslexic font uses unique weighted characters to prevent rotating or flipping letters in the mind.
- **Customizable Layouts:** You can toggle high-contrast modes, font sizing, and dyslexia-friendly typography directly from the accessibility panel.
- **Screen Reader optimization:** We are continuously improving our ARIA labels and structure to support screen readers.

Once fully rolled out, you will be able to enable these options globally in your settings panel.
