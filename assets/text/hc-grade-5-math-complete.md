## Grade 5 Mathematics Complete Curriculum & Interactive Worksheets

The **Grade 5 Mathematics Curriculum** (Level G) delivers comprehensive, rigorous coverage of upper elementary mathematics foundations aligned with EngageNY / Eureka Math and national Common Core standards. It is tailored for 5th-grade students, homeschool educators, and classroom teachers seeking structured, distraction-free mathematics mastery.

### Core Architecture & Coverage
Grade 5 features **149 sequential lessons** organized across 6 foundational modules:
- **Module 1: Place Value and Decimal Fractions (16 Lessons):** Multiplicative patterns on the place value chart, decimal fractions in expanded/unit/word forms, rounding decimals, adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing decimals.
- **Module 2: Multi-Digit Whole Number and Decimal Fraction Operations (29 Lessons):** Mental strategies for multi-digit multiplication, standard algorithm, decimal multiplication, partial quotients, and decimal division word problems.
- **Module 3: Addition and Subtraction of Fractions (16 Lessons):** Equivalent fractions, making like units pictorially with rectangular fraction models, making like units numerically, and word problem applications.
- **Module 4: Multiplication and Division of Fractions and Decimal Fractions (33 Lessons):** Line plots of fraction measurements, fractions as division, multiplication of whole numbers and fractions, scaling, fraction division, and interpreting expressions.
- **Module 5: Addition and Multiplication with Volume and Area (21 Lessons):** Concepts of volume, unit cubes, volume formulas ($V = l \times w \times h$, $V = b \times h$), area of rectangular figures with fractional side lengths, and 2D shape classifications.
- **Module 6: Problem Solving with the Coordinate Plane (34 Lessons):** Coordinate systems, patterns in the coordinate plane, graphing rules, drawing geometric figures, multi-step word problems, and reflection on elementary mathematics.

### Interactive Worksheet-First Experience
Every lesson in Grade 5 provides a clean, focused, distraction-free worksheet layout:
- **Direct Worksheet Display:** Pure focus on authentic problem set exercises, eliminating external links and research distractions.
- **Auto-Saving Digital Scratchpads:** Interactive textareas for every problem that persist student work in `localStorage` and synchronize with the cloud.
- **Teacher Solution Keys:** Instant expandable solution accordions displaying step-by-step mathematical reasoning and answers.
- **1-Click Browser Printing:**
  - **Print Blank:** Outputs cleanly formatted, paper-based worksheets with student name lines and ruled handwriting boxes.
  - **Print Solved Work:** Formats typed student notes and solutions into a completed homework packet ready for submission.

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Action Controls
- `Tab` / `Shift + Tab`: Seamlessly navigate through problem cards, action buttons, and digital scratchpad fields.
- `Space` / `Enter`: Expand and collapse teacher solution accordions and toggle print modes.
- `Ctrl + P` (or `Cmd + P`): Direct trigger for high-fidelity print output.
- `Esc`: Dismiss modal overlays and exit ticket assessment runners.

### Accessibility & UDL Compliance
- **Universal MathJax SVG Rendering:** Every mathematical formula and symbol scales dynamically, supports screen magnification up to 400%, and inherits high-contrast foreground color (`currentColor`).
- **WCAG AA/AAA Color Independence:** Meets strict contrast requirements in Light, Midnight Dark, Sepia, and AAA High-Contrast presets.
- **Assistive Technology Optimization:** Every problem card, solution accordion, and input area is equipped with semantic ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label`, `aria-controls`).
- **Offline Resiliency:** Fully cached by the PWA service worker; all 6 modules and interactive practice worksheets remain completely accessible without an internet connection.
