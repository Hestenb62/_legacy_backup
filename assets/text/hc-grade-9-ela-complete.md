## Grade 9 English Language Arts: Master Curriculum Guide

### Executive Overview
The Grade 9 English Language Arts curriculum on Hesten's Learning represents a complete 153-lesson sequence converted from the authentic EngageNY / Core Knowledge curriculum into high-performance, modular JSON datasets. Covering four comprehensive modules, the curriculum balances foundational literary analysis, psychological and classical perspectives, scientific inquiry research, and historical argument writing.

### 4-Module Scope & Sequence (153 Lessons)
* **Module 1: Reading Closely and Writing to Learn (48 Lessons):**
  * *Unit 1:* Karen Russell's *St. Lucy's Home for Girls Raised by Wolves* (17 lessons)
  * *Unit 2:* Rainer Maria Rilke's *Letters to a Young Poet* & David Mitchell's *Black Swan Green* (11 lessons)
  * *Unit 3:* William Shakespeare's *The Tragedy of Romeo and Juliet* (20 lessons)
* **Module 2: Understanding Perspectives and Writing to Convince (46 Lessons):**
  * *Unit 1:* Edgar Allan Poe's *The Tell-Tale Heart* & Emily Dickinson's *I felt a Funeral, in my Brain* (13 lessons)
  * *Unit 2:* Sophocles' *Oedipus the King* (20 lessons)
  * *Unit 3:* Walter Mosley's *True Crime: The Roots of an American Obsession* (13 lessons)
* **Module 3: Researching to Deepen Understanding (30 Lessons):**
  * *Unit 1:* Temple Grandin's *Animals in Translation* Seed Text Analysis (10 lessons)
  * *Unit 2:* Inquiry-Based Pre-Searching & Source Evaluation (12 lessons)
  * *Unit 3:* Evidence Synthesis & Research Paper Drafting (8 lessons)
* **Module 4: Sugar Changed the World & Historical Argument (29 Lessons):**
  * Marc Aronson & Marina Budhos' *Sugar Changed the World* and World Bank *Globalization* Studies (29 lessons)

### Student Features & Accessibility
* **Paragraph Anchors:** Each paragraph possesses an explicit `#p1`, `#p2`... anchor ID for instant reference during close-reading analysis.
* **Web Speech Read-Aloud:** High-fidelity browser text-to-speech synthesis with Play, Pause, and Stop controls.
* **Dyslexia & Low-Vision Support:** OpenDyslexic typeface switcher, zoom magnification, and high-contrast color scheme compatibility.
* **Worksheet-First Practice:** Distraction-free problem sets with auto-saving textareas, expandable teacher solution keys, and 1-click printing.
