## Academic Research Portal & Peer-Reviewed Journals (/research/index.php)

Explore the empirical learning science, cognitive neurobiology, and evidence-based pedagogical frameworks underpinning the instructional architecture of Hesten's Learning. The Research Portal hosts peer-reviewed open-access journals and technical monographs on developmental learning differences.

### Quick Access & Navigation
- **Direct Web Endpoint:** Visit `/research/index.php` from your browser or PWA.
- **Top Header & Footer:** Click **Research** in the navigation bar or under the Quick Links footer column.
- **Command Palette:** Press `Ctrl + K` and type `research`, `dldr`, `dyslexia`, `dysgraphia`, or `kinematics`.
- **Lesson Pedagogical Links:** Click the **Evidence-Based Pedagogy & Research** badge directly in curriculum lesson overviews.

### Peer-Reviewed Journal Publications
1. **Dyslexia & Learning Disabilities Research (DLDR - /research/DLDR/):**
   - **Scope:** Neurobiological foundations of reading acquisition, phonological decoding mechanisms, developmental dyslexia subtypes, and structured literacy interventions.
   - **Featured Publications:**
     - *Phonological Awareness & Rhyming Impact in Early Childhood Interventions*: An empirical study on phoneme segmentation training, phonological working memory, and orthographic mapping.
     - *DLDR Editorial Scope & Methodological Standards*: Open science methodologies, peer review guidelines, and accessibility in academic publishing.

2. **Dysgraphia Studies & Motor Skills (DSMS - /research/DSMS/):**
   - **Scope:** Graphomotor impairments, fine motor kinematics, proprioceptive feedback, neural handwriting circuitry, and classroom occupational therapy accommodations.
   - **Featured Publications:**
     - *Classroom Accommodations for Dysgraphia*: Environmental modifications, digital scribe technologies, speech-to-text dictation, and untimed testing protocols.
     - *Fine Motor Kinematics in Dysgraphic Writers*: Quantitative spatial velocity, stroke duration, pen grip pressure, and trajectory variability during handwriting tasks.
     - *DSMS Scope & Methodological Standards*: Biomechanical tracking methods, kinematic tablet capture protocols, and clinical standards.

### Universal Offline Access & PWA Integration
- **100% Offline Capability:** All journals, abstracts, full-text markdown manuscripts, and cover assets are pre-cached by the Service Worker (`hestens-learning-v21`), enabling research access even with zero internet connectivity.
- **Markdown & Scholarly Typography:** Rendered with semantic academic typography, accessible footnotes, citations, and high-contrast tables.

### Multimodal Accessibility & UDL Features
- **Speech Synthesis (TTS):** Read entire research articles aloud with browser speech synthesis.
- **Adaptive Typography:** Full compatibility with OpenDyslexic typeface, high-contrast AAA themes (7:1 contrast), and Irlen color filter overlays.
- **Keyboard Navigation:** 100% keyboard operable across all journal directories and article readers.
