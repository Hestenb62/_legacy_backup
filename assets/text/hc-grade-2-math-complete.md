## Grade 2 Mathematics Complete Curriculum & Interactive Worksheets

The **Grade 2 Mathematics Curriculum** (Level D) establishes robust numerical foundations, place value fluency, and algebraic reasoning aligned with EngageNY / Eureka Math and national Common Core standards. Students build number sense through hands-on base ten manipulatives, number bonds, tape diagrams, and open number lines.

### Core Architecture & Coverage
Grade 2 features **149 sequential lessons** organized across 8 essential modules:
- **Module 1: Sums and Differences to 100 (8 Lessons):** Fluency with addition and subtraction within 20, making a ten, and addition and subtraction of multiples of 10 and two-digit numbers.
- **Module 2: Addition and Subtraction of Length Units (10 Lessons):** Metric measurement with centimeters and meters, comparing lengths, measuring on a ruler, and solving word problems involving length.
- **Module 3: Place Value, Counting, and Comparison of Numbers to 1,000 (21 Lessons):** Base-ten blocks, bundles of hundreds, tens, and ones, counting to 1,000, reading and writing numbers in standard, expanded, and word forms, and comparing numbers using $<$, $>$, and $=$.
- **Module 4: Addition and Subtraction Within 200 with Word Problems to 100 (31 Lessons):** Vertical form addition and subtraction, decomposing and composing tens and hundreds, chip models and place value charts, and two-step word problems.
- **Module 5: Addition and Subtraction Within 1,000 with Word Problems to 100 (20 Lessons):** Multi-digit addition and subtraction with composing and decomposing across tens and hundreds, mental math strategies, and algorithm verification.
- **Module 6: Foundations of Multiplication and Division (20 Lessons):** Equal groups of objects, repeated addition, arrays of up to 5 rows and 5 columns, even and odd number patterns, and rectangular grid partitioning.
- **Module 7: Problem Solving with Length, Money, and Data (26 Lessons):** Measuring length in customary units (inches, feet, yards), line plots, identifying coins and bills, calculating values, and multi-step word problems involving money.
- **Module 8: Time, Shapes, and Fractions as Equal Parts of Shapes (13 Lessons):** Telling time to the nearest 5 minutes (a.m./p.m.), attributes of 2D and 3D shapes (vertices, sides, angles), and partitioning circles and rectangles into halves, thirds, and fourths.

### Interactive Worksheet-First Experience
Every lesson in Grade 2 provides a clean, focused, distraction-free worksheet layout:
- **Direct Worksheet Display:** Pure focus on authentic problem set exercises, eliminating external links and research distractions.
- **Auto-Saving Digital Scratchpads:** Interactive textareas for every problem that persist student work in `localStorage` and synchronize with the cloud.
- **Teacher Solution Keys:** Instant expandable solution accordions displaying step-by-step mathematical reasoning and answers.
- **1-Click Browser Printing:**
  - **Print Blank:** Outputs cleanly formatted, paper-based worksheets with student name lines and ruled handwriting boxes.
  - **Print Solved Work:** Formats typed student notes and solutions into a completed homework packet ready for submission.

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Action Controls
- `Tab` / `Shift + Tab`: Seamlessly navigate through problem cards, action buttons, and digital scratchpad fields.
- `Space` / `Enter`: Expand and collapse teacher solution accordions and toggle print modes.
- `Ctrl + P` (or `Cmd + P`): Direct trigger for high-fidelity print output.
- `Esc`: Dismiss modal overlays and exit ticket assessment runners.

### Accessibility & UDL Compliance
- **Universal MathJax SVG Rendering:** Every mathematical formula and symbol scales dynamically, supports screen magnification up to 400%, and inherits high-contrast foreground color (`currentColor`).
- **WCAG AA/AAA Color Independence:** Meets strict contrast requirements in Light, Midnight Dark, Sepia, and AAA High-Contrast presets.
- **Assistive Technology Optimization:** Every problem card, solution accordion, and input area is equipped with semantic ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label`, `aria-controls`).
- **Offline Resiliency:** Fully cached by the PWA service worker; all 8 modules and interactive practice worksheets remain completely accessible without an internet connection.
