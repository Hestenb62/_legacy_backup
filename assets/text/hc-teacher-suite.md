## Educator & Homeschool Suite (Teacher Hub)

The **Educator & Homeschool Suite** ([`pages/teachers.php`](/pages/teachers.php)) is a streamlined teaching workstation designed for educators, homeschool families, and academic tutors. Redesigned with a focused **Modern Academic Studio** architecture, it provides interactive assignment builders, multi-grade curriculum pacing guides, an automated lesson plan generator, and printable educator resources in a unified, accessible workspace.

### Core Workstation Tools

#### 1. Interactive Assignment Link Builder
- **2-Column Responsive Layout:** Divides parameters (Grade, Subject, Standard Target, Assessment Mode, Question Count) on the left from the sticky live URL output and LMS prompt generator on the right.
- **Direct Assignment URLs:** Instantly creates student launch URLs targeting specific Common Core (CCSS), Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS), and College, Career, and Civic Life (C3) standards.
- **Fluency Sprint Dispatcher:** Toggle between Standard Mastery practice (untimed with full pedagogical rationales) and 60-Second Fluency Sprints (rapid speed recall).
- **LMS Prompt Generator:** One-click copy formatted text prompts for Google Classroom, Canvas, Schoology, or direct messaging with assignment instructions and links.
- **Student Preview Mode:** Launch a simulated student session in a separate tab to verify question difficulty and scoring.

#### 2. Master Curriculum Pacing Guide
- **Universal Multi-Grade Coverage:** Full curriculum sequences for all 11 grade levels (Pre-K through High School).
- **Dual Structural Scheduling:** Toggle between the classic 36-Week 4-Quarter calendar and the 48-Week Year-Round Fiscal Wave structure (6 Waves • 8 Weeks each with 7 weeks instruction + 1 week synthesis).
- **Unified Pacing Command Bar:** Consolidated controls for grade level selection, schedule models, quarter/wave filters, and 1-click printable syllabus binders.
- **Wave Week Playbook & Differentiation Engine:** Deep-dive modal with daily Monday–Friday breakdowns, Lexile-aligned literature suggestions, instructional strategies, and printable weekly blueprints.

#### 3. Standards-Aligned Lesson Plan Customizer
- **Complete Pedagogical Architecture:** Automatically drafts 5E instructional lesson plans including learning objectives, essential questions, materials checklist, academic vocabulary, direct instruction, guided practice, independent practice, and exit ticket formative checks.
- **Printable 2-Page Handouts:** Generates clean, printer-friendly student worksheets and matching teacher guides with answer keys.

#### 4. Curated Educator Resources & Dedicated Tools
- **Official Mastery Diplomas:** Format and print landscape achievement certificates with credential IDs and gold seals for homeschool portfolios.
- **Standards Navigator:** Direct shortcut to browse K–12 standards across all domains.
- **Dedicated Creative Studios:** Word Search and Crossword Puzzle generators are available as standalone tools via [`pages/word-search.php`](/pages/word-search.php) and [`pages/crossword.php`](/pages/crossword.php) for dedicated, distraction-free worksheet creation.

### Accessibility, WCAG AAA & UDL Compliance
- **100% Keyboard Operability:** Complete navigation through `Tab`, `Shift+Tab`, `Enter`, `Space`, and `Esc` on all forms, tabs, and modal dialogs.
- **WCAG AAA High Contrast:** High-contrast mode provides ≥7:1 text contrast with crisp high-visibility borders and gold highlights.
- **Screen Reader Semantics:** Clear landmark roles (`role="tablist"`, `role="tab"`, `role="tabpanel"`, `role="dialog"`, `aria-live="polite"`).
- **Universal Design for Learning (UDL):** Multimodal outputs, untimed modes, dyslexia-friendly fonts, and adjustable pacing profiles.
