## ELA Literature Interactive Study Modals & Narrative Vaults

The **English Language Arts & Literature Resource Terminal** provides students and educators with comprehensive, curriculum-aligned literary analysis lessons, structural narrative formulas, visual graphic organizers, and formative practice checks across 6 fundamental literature domains and 24 specialized topics:

1. **Literary Devices**: Metaphor & Simile, Imagery & Symbolism, Foreshadowing & Flashback, Irony & Satire
2. **Character Analysis**: Protagonist & Antagonist, Character Archetypes, Character Development Arc, Character vs. Conflict
3. **Theme Exploration**: Identifying Themes, Universal Themes, Symbolism & Theme, Author's Message
4. **Genre Studies**: Fiction Genres, Non-Fiction Genres, Poetry Forms, Drama & Playwriting
5. **Plot & Structure**: Freytag's Pyramid, Conflict Types, Pacing & Suspense, Narrative Arcs
6. **Author's Craft**: Diction & Syntax, Tone & Mood, Point of View, Imagery & Sensory Details

### Core Features & Capabilities

- **Interactive Comprehensive Study Modals**: Clicking any literature topic card or badge launches a rich, accessible learning modal:
  - **Syntax Rules & Structural Formulations Vault**: Labeled narratological formulas, tenor-vehicle mappings, Freytag tension functions, and Genette pacing ratios typeset via crisp MathJax SVG notation.
  - **Visual Maps & Structural Graphic Organizers**: Handcrafted vector diagrams (Monomyth Archetypal Wheel, Conflict Matrix, Symbolic Iceberg, Freytag's Pyramid, Point of View Vantage Points, and Sensory Immersion Wheel).
  - **Curated Educational References & Academic Guides**: Direct links to Common Core Standards, Folger Shakespeare Library, Poetry Foundation, Joseph Campbell Foundation, Harvard Comparative Literature, and Purdue OWL.
  - **Concept Lecture & Subject Breakdown**: Rigorous academic definitions broken down into 3 structural pillars.
  - **Dual-Lexile Readability Toggle**: Instantly switch between *Standard Academic Lexile* and *Basic English (Simplified)* without losing context.
  - **Real-World Examples & Literary Excerpts**: Textual analyses of classical and contemporary world literature.
  - **Pro-Tips & Analysis Strategies**: Practical analytical techniques (e.g. tracking character epiphanies, the Hitchcock suspense principle, the Rule of Three for symbols).
  - **Formative Practice Checks**: Immediate self-assessment questions with instant validation and explanatory breakdowns.
- **Universal MathJax SVG Typography**: All syntax rules and narrative formulations render with high contrast, crisp vector fidelity, and theme inheritance across light and dark modes.
- **Real-Time Topic Search & Domain Filters**: Filter literature topics dynamically by typing keywords or clicking domain filter badges.

### Accessibility & Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

- **Multimodal Audio Read-Aloud**: Integrated Text-to-Speech (TTS) via the "Listen" button articulates definitions, examples, and rules.
- **Full Keyboard Navigation**: Complete modal focus management, `Escape` key dismissal, `Tab` cycling, and `Enter`/`Space` choice selection.
- **Dyslexia & Sensory Support**: Full compatibility with OpenDyslexic font toggling, Irlen color tints, bionic reading, and AAA high-contrast modes.
- **Responsive Vector Organizers**: Every diagram is an accessible SVG equipped with `role="img"`, descriptive `aria-label`, and fluid container scaling.
