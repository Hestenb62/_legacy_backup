### Collection Features & Highlights
1. **The Declaration of Independence (Thomas Jefferson & Continental Congress, eBook #1):**
   - **Historical Significance:** Project Gutenberg's very first electronic book, transcribed by Michael S. Hart in July 1971.
   - **3 Dedicated Chapters:**
     - *Chapter 1:* Introduction & Historical Origins (Xerox Sigma V mainframe origins, transcription notes).
     - *Chapter 2:* The Unanimous Declaration of Independence (Complete text, preamble, bill of 27 grievances against King George III, signers grouped by state).
     - *Chapter 3:* Thomas Jefferson's Handwritten Drafts & Notes (Early draft engravings and Franklin/Adams emendations).

2. **The United States Bill of Rights (Amendments I–X, eBook #2):**
   - **2 Dedicated Chapters:**
     - *Chapter 1:* Preamble & Amendments I–V (Religious freedom, speech, press, assembly, right to bear arms, quartering, search & seizure, grand jury, due process, eminent domain).
     - *Chapter 2:* Amendments VI–X (Speedy trial, civil jury, cruel and unusual punishment, unenumerated rights, states' and people's reserved powers).

3. **The United States Constitution (Constitutional Convention 1787, eBook #5):**
   - **7 Dedicated Chapters (One per Article):**
     - *Chapter 1:* Preamble & Article I (The Legislative Branch, House, Senate, Congressional powers).
     - *Chapter 2:* Article II (The Executive Branch, Presidential powers, Electoral College, Commander in Chief, Impeachment).
     - *Chapter 3:* Article III (The Judicial Branch, Supreme Court, Treason).
     - *Chapter 4:* Article IV (States' Relations, Full Faith and Credit, Privileges & Immunities).
     - *Chapter 5:* Article V (The Amendment Process).
     - *Chapter 6:* Article VI (National Supremacy, Debts, Oaths).
     - *Chapter 7:* Article VII & The Signers (Ratification procedure and signatures of the Framers).

4. **Give Me Liberty or Give Me Death (Patrick Henry, eBook #6):**
   - **Historical Context:** Delivered at St. John's Church in Richmond, Virginia on March 23, 1775.
   - **2 Dedicated Chapters:**
     - *Chapter 1:* Historical Setting & The Second Virginia Convention.
     - *Chapter 2:* The Complete Oration with integrated streaming LibriVox audio recitation.

5. **Give Me Liberty or Give Me Death (Audio Edition, eBook #20046):**
   - Embedded HTML5 audio player streaming public domain LibriVox MP3 and OGG recordings.
   - Synchronized speech transcript for multisensory auditory and visual reading.

### Accessibility & Universal Design for Learning (UDL)
- **Multimodal Learning:** Read along while listening to either synthesized Text-to-Speech narration or human-narrated historic recordings.
- **Customizable Typography:** Adjust font sizing, line height, and toggle OpenDyslexic, Atkinson Hyperlegible, or Bionic Reading.
- **Distraction-Free Zen Mode:** Press `Z` or `Esc` to focus purely on constitutional text.
- **Offline Reading:** Save any civics document directly to device storage for offline classroom study.
