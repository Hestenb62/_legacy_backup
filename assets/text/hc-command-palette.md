## Global Command Palette & Instant Jump (Ctrl + K)

Navigate anywhere across Hesten's Learning in milliseconds! The Global Command Palette provides a unified keyboard-first search, voice search, and action launcher accessible from any page on the platform.

### Quick Access & Shortcuts
- **Keyboard Shortcut:** Press `Ctrl + K` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd + K` (Mac) anywhere on the site.
- **Search Header Bar:** Click the search box located in the top navigation header.
- **Escape Key:** Press `Esc` at any time to instantly dismiss the palette and return focus to your previous position.

### Core Superpowers & Filter Modes
- **Instant Search:** Type any keyword, subject, grade level, or book title to instantly find pages, tools, and learning materials.
- **Speech-to-Text Voice Jump:** Click the microphone icon or press `Alt + V` inside the search bar to dictate your search query using the Web Speech API.
- **Category Filter Pills:** Click or arrow over to filter results by All, Pages, Skills Tree, Learning Tools, or Curriculum Standards.
- **Quick Command Shortcuts (Type `>`):**
  - `> openScratchpad` or `> scratchpad`: Launch the Unified Multi-Note Scratchpad.
  - `> openWhiteboard` or `> whiteboard`: Open the interactive math drawing canvas.
  - `> openTimer` or `> timer`: Launch the Pomodoro study timer.
  - `> openSensory` or `> sensory`: Open the Sensory Reset & Breathing Chamber.
  - `> toggleContrast`: Switch between standard theme and high-contrast accessibility mode.
  - `> startDictation`: Begin speech-to-text voice input.
- **Keyboard Arrow Navigation:** Use `ArrowDown` and `ArrowUp` to scroll through matching results, and press `Enter` to navigate directly.

### Universal Accessibility & UDL
- **Screen Reader Announcements:** Dynamic result counts and active item highlights are announced via `aria-live="polite"`.
- **High Contrast Focus Rings:** Unmistakable 3px gold focus indicators outline the active search result.
- **Offline Resiliency:** All site endpoints and tool actions operate 100% offline without external network queries.
