### Chronological Archive Overview

#### Volume I: 1848–1861 (Stanton, Anthony, Gage — 20 Chapters)
- **The Origins of the Movement:** Covers preceding causes, women in journalism, the World's Anti-Slavery Convention in London (1840), the historic Seneca Falls Convention of 1848, the Rochester Convention, and early state assemblies across Ohio, Indiana, Pennsylvania, and Massachusetts.
- **Key Primary Records:** The Declaration of Sentiments, early speeches of Lucretia Mott, Elizabeth Cady Stanton, and Frederick Douglass.

#### Volume II: 1861–1876 (Stanton, Anthony, Gage — 15 Chapters)
- **Civil War, Reconstruction & Constitutional Debates:** Explores women's wartime service, the Women's Loyal National League petition drive for emancipation, Congressional debates surrounding the 14th and 15th Amendments, the founding of the National Woman Suffrage Association (NWSA) and the American Woman Suffrage Association (AWSA), trials of Susan B. Anthony for voting, and the 1876 Centennial Declaration of Rights of the Women of the United States.

#### Volume III: 1876–1885 (Anthony, Gage, Stanton — 66 Chapters)
- **National Conventions & State Referenda:** Detailed proceedings of the NWSA conventions in Washington, D.C., Senate judiciary committee hearings, state suffrage campaigns in Nebraska, Kansas, and Iowa, and legal appeals asserting women's citizenship privileges.

#### Volume IV: 1883–1900 (Anthony, Harper — 82 Chapters)
- **The NAWSA Merger & Western Victories:** Documents the historic merger of the NWSA and AWSA into the National American Woman Suffrage Association (NAWSA) in 1890, annual conventions, speeches by Susan B. Anthony, Carrie Chapman Catt, and Anna Howard Shaw, and the successful enfranchisement of women in Wyoming, Colorado, Utah, and Idaho.

#### Volume V: 1900–1920 (Harper — 30 Chapters)
- **The Federal Campaign & 19th Amendment:** Details the climactic national struggle for the Susan B. Anthony Amendment under the leadership of Carrie Chapman Catt and Maud Wood Park, national suffrage parades, wartime contributions during World War I, Congressional floor debates, and final passage in the House and Senate in June 1919.

#### Volume VI: 1900–1920 (Harper — 58 Chapters)
- **The State-by-State Record:** A comprehensive state-by-state encyclopedic record detailing the grassroots suffrage associations, legislative lobbying, popular referenda, and ratification battles across every state from Alabama to Wyoming, plus Alaska, Hawaii, the Philippines, and international suffrage alliances.
