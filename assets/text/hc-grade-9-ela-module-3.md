## Grade 9 ELA Module 3: Researching to Deepen Understanding

### Executive Overview
Grade 9 ELA Module 3 immerses high school scholars in an authentic, iterative research methodology centered around Temple Grandin and Catherine Johnson's groundbreaking seed text, *Animals in Translation: Using the Mysteries of Autism to Decode Animal Behavior*. Across 30 rigorously structured lessons, students cultivate independent inquiry paths, learn how to pre-search and vet credible sources, construct structured research frames, and compose a multi-paragraph, evidence-based research paper.

### Curriculum Structure & Unit Breakdown
* **Unit 1: Seed Text Analysis (Lessons 1–10):** Close examination of *Animals in Translation*, exploring how Grandin's unique perspective as an autistic scientist provides insights into animal cognition and behavior. Students annotate passages, generate text-based inquiry questions, and identify areas of investigation.
* **Unit 2: Inquiry-Based Research & Source Evaluation (Lessons 1–12):** Independent and guided research using the Area Evaluation Checklist, Potential Sources Tool, and Taking Notes Tool. Students assess credibility, bias, and relevance across academic and media resources.
* **Unit 3: Organizing Evidence & Synthesis (Lessons 1–8):** Synthesizing claims into a cohesive Evidence-Based Perspective, drafting outlines, evaluating claim-evidence alignment, and composing the final research paper with formal conventions and MLA documentation.

### Core Student Capabilities & Study Tools
* **Interactive Close-Reading Worksheets:** Formative text-dependent questions and synthesizing Quick Writes embedded directly into each lesson page.
