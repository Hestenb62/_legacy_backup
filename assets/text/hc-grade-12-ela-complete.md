## Grade 12 English Language Arts (Level N): Full EngageNY Curriculum & High School Capstone

### Executive Overview
Grade 12 English Language Arts (Level N) represents the collegiate-preparatory capstone of secondary English education on Hesten's Learning platform. Spanning 154 comprehensive lessons across 4 core EngageNY modules, Level N challenges senior students to master autobiographical narrative craft, political philosophy, Roman tragic drama, global environmental history, and modern cross-cultural identity.

With the completion of Grade 12, **High School English Language Arts (Grades 9–12) is 100% complete across all 16 modules, comprising 598 total lessons** and 43 dedicated Digital Library reading pages.

---

### Core Curriculum Modules

#### Module 1: Personal Narrative, Cultural Heritage, and Self-Definition (41 Lessons)
*Theme: How Do Authors Use Style, Structure, and Voice to Shape Identity and Life Experiences?*
- **Topic A (Lessons 1–28): Self-Transformation & Racial Justice**
  - Malcolm X (with Alex Haley): *The Autobiography of Malcolm X*
  - *Focus: Evolution of Malcolm X's worldview, systemic racism in mid-century America, prison education, Mecca pilgrimage, and rhetorical voice.*
- **Topic B (Lessons 29–34): Pueblo Heritage & Cultural Aesthetics**
  - Leslie Marmon Silko: *"Yellow Woman and a Beauty of the Spirit"*
  - *Focus: Laguna Pueblo traditions, communal harmony as spiritual beauty, indigenous storytelling, and cultural survival.*
- **Topic C (Lessons 35–41): College Application Narrative Writing Workshop**
  - Common Application Prompts & Narrative Craft
  - *Focus: Drafting, peer review, and polishing authentic personal essays for higher education and professional portfolios.*

#### Module 2: Civil Resistance, Power, and Roman Tragedy (38 Lessons)
*Theme: The Boundaries of State Authority, Civic Duty, and Political Conspiracies*
- **Topic A (Lessons 1–16): Rhetoric of Resistance**
  - Benazir Bhutto: *"Ideas Live On"* (2007)
  - Henry David Thoreau: *Civil Disobedience* (1849)
  - *Focus: Democratic legitimacy vs. tyranny, conscience as higher law, passive vs. active resistance, and paying the poll tax.*
- **Topic B (Lessons 17–38): Power, Conspiracy & Tragic Downfall**
  - William Shakespeare: *The Tragedy of Julius Caesar*
  - *Focus: Brutus's internal conflict, Cassius's manipulation, Antony's funeral oration, mob psychology, and the collapse of the Roman Republic.*

#### Module 3: Anthropological Inquiry and Global Argumentation (38 Lessons)
*Theme: Environmental Determinants of Human History & Research Synthesis*
- **Topic A (Lessons 1–27): Seed Text & Inquiry Formulation**
  - Jared Diamond: *Guns, Germs, and Steel: The Fates of Human Societies*
  - *Focus: Yali's question, continental axes, plant and animal domestication, epidemic microbes, and environmental determinism.*
- **Topic B (Lessons 28–38): Evidence-Based Argument Writing**
  - *Focus: Gathering, vetting, and synthesizing academic sources into an authoritative, cited research-based argument essay.*

#### Module 4: Desire, Illusion, and Cross-Cultural Identity (37 Lessons)
*Theme: Power Dynamics, Nostalgia, Alienation, and Generational Heritage*
- **Topic A (Lessons 1–14): Power, Illusion & Rebirth**
  - Tennessee Williams: *A Streetcar Named Desire*
  - Jimmy Santiago Baca: *"A Daily Joy to Be Alive"*
  - *Focus: Blanche DuBois vs. Stanley Kowalski, Southern aristocracy vs. modern industrialism, and poetic resilience.*
- **Topic B (Lessons 15–37): Alienation & The Names We Carry**
  - Nikolai Gogol: *"The Overcoat"*
  - Jhumpa Lahiri: *The Namesake*
  - *Focus: Akaky Akakievich's petty clerk tragedy, Bengali naming traditions, Gogol Ganguli's immigrant identity, and generational reconciliation.*

### Interactive Features & Tools
- **Digital Library Anchor Links:** Every lesson features direct access opening the corresponding reading in the Digital Library (`/library/?page=<slug>`).
- **Close Reading Question Sets:** Deep text-dependent analytical questions examining authorial diction, rhetoric, and dramatic technique.
- **Synthesizing Quick Writes:** Structured writing prompts evaluated against Common Core 11–12 rubrics.
- **Auto-Saving Response Fields:** Student work is continuously preserved in browser `localStorage`.
- **Teacher Answer Keys:** Worked exemplar solutions with evidence citations and scoring guides.
- **1-Click Printing:** `printProblemSet()` formats clean worksheets for offline binders and classroom submissions.

---

### Accessibility & UDL Features
- **WCAG AA/AAA Compliance:** 100% keyboard navigable (`Tab`, `Shift+Tab`, `Enter`, `Space`, `Esc`).
- **High-Contrast Focus Indicators:** Theme-adaptive `:focus-visible` focus rings.
- **Universal Design for Learning:** OpenDyslexic font support, adjustable text sizing, line spacing, Irlen tints, and speech read-aloud tools.
- **Offline Resiliency:** Full curriculum data and reading pages function completely offline via service worker caching.
