# Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973
**Statutory Citation**: 29 U.S.C. § 794d (As Amended)  
**Regulatory Standards**: 36 C.F.R. Part 1194 (U.S. Access Board ICT Final Rule)  
**Federal Enforceability**: Federal Agencies, Public Schools (via ADA Title II & Section 504), Assistive Tech Mandates

---

## 1. Statutory Mandate (29 U.S.C. § 794d)

### Subsection (a) Requirements for Federal Departments and Agencies
#### (1) Accessibility
When developing, procuring, maintaining, or using electronic and information technology, each Federal department or agency, including the United States Postal Service, shall ensure, unless an undue burden would be imposed on the department or agency, that the electronic and information technology allows, regardless of the type of medium of the technology:
* **(A) Individuals with disabilities who are Federal employees** to have access to and use of information and data that is comparable to the access to and use of the information and data by Federal employees who are not individuals with disabilities; and
* **(B) Individuals with disabilities who are members of the public** seeking information or services from a Federal department or agency to have access to and use of information and data that is comparable to the access to and use of the information and data by such members of the public who are not individuals with disabilities.

#### (2) Alternative Means of Access
When development, procurement, maintenance, or use of electronic and information technology that meets the standards published by the Access Board under section 508 would pose an undue burden, the Federal department or agency shall provide individuals with disabilities covered by paragraph (1) with the information and data involved by an alternative means of access that allows the individual to use the information and data.

## 2. Revised Section 508 Standards (36 C.F.R. § 1194)

The Revised Section 508 Standards refresh and reorganize the federal electronic and information technology standards into chapters based on the type of information and communication technology (ICT):

### Chapter 1: Application and Administration
* **E101.1 Purpose**: These Revised 508 Standards provide regulations for electronic and information technology procured, developed, maintained, or used by Federal agencies pursuant to Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended.
* **E102.1 Equivalent Facilitation**: The use of an alternative design or technology that results in substantially equivalent or greater accessibility and usability by individuals with disabilities than would be provided by strict compliance with the provisions of these standards is permitted.

### Chapter 2: Scoping Requirements
* **E205.1 Electronic Content General**: Public-facing electronic content, and non-public-facing electronic content that communicates agency announcements, education or training materials, forms, questionnaires, or emergency notices, shall conform to the accessibility requirements specified in E205.4.
* **E205.4 Accessibility Standards (WCAG Incorporation)**: Electronic content shall conform to Level A and Level AA Success Criteria and Conformance Requirements in WCAG 2.0 (incorporated by reference in Chapter 1).

## 3. Subpart C: Functional Performance Criteria (§ 1194.31)

Where the technical standards do not address one or more features of ICT, the following functional performance criteria apply to ensure that individuals with disabilities can achieve the same educational and functional outcomes:

* **302.1 Without Vision**: Where a visual mode of operation is provided, ICT shall provide at least one mode of operation that does not require user vision.
* **302.2 With Limited Vision**: Where a visual mode of operation is provided, ICT shall provide at least one mode of operation that enables users to make use of limited vision (magnification, contrast, customizable typography).
* **302.3 Without Perception of Color**: Where a visual mode of operation is provided, ICT shall provide at least one visual mode of operation that does not require user perception of color.
* **302.4 Without Hearing**: Where an audible mode of operation is provided, ICT shall provide at least one mode of operation that does not require user hearing.
* **302.5 With Limited Hearing**: Where an audible mode of operation is provided, ICT shall provide at least one mode of operation that enables users to make use of limited hearing (volume boost, noise reduction).
* **302.6 Without Speech**: Where speech is used for input, control, or operation, ICT shall provide at least one mode of operation that does not require speech.
* **302.7 With Limited Manipulation**: Where a manual mode of operation is provided, ICT shall provide at least one mode of operation that does not require fine motor control or simultaneous manual operations (keyboard navigation, single-switch support).
* **302.8 With Limited Reach and Strength**: Where a manual mode of operation is provided, ICT shall provide at least one mode of operation that is operable with limited reach and limited strength.
* **302.9 With Limited Language, Cognitive, and Learning Abilities**: ICT shall provide features making its use by individuals with limited cognitive, language, and learning abilities simpler and easier (accommodations for dyslexia, ADHD, working memory deficit, and cognitive pacing).

## 4. Application to Educational Technology & Public Learning
Under joint guidance issued by the **U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ)** and the **U.S. Department of Education (ED) Office for Civil Rights (OCR)**:
* School districts, charter schools, and state educational agencies receiving federal assistance are prohibited by **Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act** and **Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)** from denying students with disabilities equal access to digital curriculum materials, student information portals, and virtual assessments.
* Hesten's Learning complies directly with Section 508 / 504 mandates by implementing assistive technology fallbacks, multi-sensory text-to-speech, OpenDyslexic letterforms, and comprehensive keyboard operability.
