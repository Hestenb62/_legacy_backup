## Grade 12 Precalculus Curriculum & Master 111-Lesson Buildout

The Grade 12 Precalculus curriculum represents a capstone high school advanced mathematics course encompassing 111 interactive lessons across five core instructional modules. Derived directly from authentic EngageNY / Eureka Math source materials, every lesson is streamlined directly around official Problem Set practice worksheets with expandable teacher solution keys, MathJax LaTeX typography, and 1-click printing.

### Curriculum Structure & Module Inventory
- **Module 1: Complex Numbers and Transformations (Lessons 1–30)**:
  - *Topic A (Lessons 1–8)*: Questions of linearity, classifying real and complex linear transformations, geometric representation of complex numbers as vectors, and complex number division using conjugates.
  - *Topic B (Lessons 9–17)*: Complex number operations as geometric transformations (translations, rotations, dilations, and reflections), modulus, distance, trigonometry, multiplying by reciprocals, and geometric proofs.
  - *Topic C (Lessons 18–30)*: Exploiting connections to trigonometry (Euler's formula $e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta$), matrix notation, 2x2 matrix operations, modeling video game motions, determinants, and matrix inverses.
- **Module 2: Vectors and Matrices (Lessons 1–27)**:
  - *Topic A (Lessons 1–3)*: Network diagrams, adjacency matrices, and matrix arithmetic in applied contexts.
  - *Topic B (Lessons 4–13)*: Linear transformations in 2D and 3D coordinate space, composition of transformations via matrix multiplication, matrix algebra properties (associativity, distributivity, non-commutativity), and cryptographic matrix encryption.
  - *Topic C (Lessons 14–16)*: Solving systems of linear equations using matrix inverses and Gaussian reduction ($Ax = b \Rightarrow x = A^{-1}b$).
  - *Topic D (Lessons 17–24)*: Vector definitions, magnitude, direction, vector addition/subtraction, scalar multiplication, parametric line equations, stone bridge force vectors, and velocity modeling.
  - *Topic E (Lessons 25–27)*: First-person 3D video game programming: 3D-to-2D perspective projection matrices and player camera transformations.
- **Module 3: Rational and Exponential Functions (Lessons 1–21)**:
  - *Topic A (Lessons 1–9)*: Polynomial equations, roots of unity on the unit circle, the Binomial Theorem, complex curves, Cavalieri's principle for volume, and the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra.
  - *Topic B (Lessons 10–17)*: Structure of rational expressions, graphing rational functions, horizontal, vertical, and slant asymptotes, and function composition.
  - *Topic C (Lessons 18–21)*: Inverse functions, restricting function domains, inverses of logarithmic and exponential functions, and analytical problem solving.
- **Module 4: Trigonometry (Lessons 1–14)**:
  - *Topic A (Lessons 1–6)*: Special triangles and unit circle symmetries, trigonometric function properties, angle addition and subtraction identities, tangent lines, and wave superposition.
  - *Topic B (Lessons 7–10)*: Trigonometry in general non-right triangles: triangle area formula ($A = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$), Law of Sines, and Law of Cosines.
  - *Topic C (Lessons 11–14)*: Graphs of trigonometric functions, inverse trigonometric functions ($\arcsin$, $\arccos$, $\arctan$), and modeling periodic phenomena with inverse trig.
- **Module 5: Probability and Statistics (Lessons 1–19)**:
  - *Topic A (Lessons 1–4)*: Combinatorics: Fundamental Counting Principle, permutations, combinations, and computing classical probabilities.
  - *Topic B (Lessons 5–12)*: Discrete random variables, probability mass functions, expected value ($E[X] = \sum x_i P(x_i)$), and empirical probability distributions.
  - *Topic C (Lessons 13–19)*: Making decisions under uncertainty: games of chance, fair games, comparing business and medical strategies using expected values, and decision tree analysis.

### Interactive Features & Study Tools
- **Embedded Problem Set Worksheets**: Every lesson presents the full authentic worksheet practice problem sets paired with step-by-step teacher solutions, eliminating external download barriers.
- **1-Click Printing**: Dedicated print stylesheets format clean, distraction-free worksheets via `printProblemSet()`.
- **Digital Student Workspace**: Built-in auto-saving textarea for working out calculations and proofs.
- **Streamlined Presentation**: Clean layout focused directly on the worksheet and mathematical derivations without extraneous links.
- **Dynamic MathJax Typography**: All matrix arrays, vector brackets, trigonometric equations, and complex plane formulas render as responsive vector SVGs.

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Action Controls
- `Tab` / `Shift+Tab`: Navigate sequentially through lesson sections, problem items, and action buttons.
- `Enter` or `Space`: Expand/collapse teacher solution keys and toggle worksheet views.
- `Alt + P`: Print the current lesson problem set worksheet.
- `Alt + B`: Bookmark the active precalculus lesson.
- `Esc`: Close open overlays.

### Accessibility & Universal Design for Learning (UDL)
- **High Contrast Focus & Vector Scalability**: Matrices and LaTeX notation adapt to light and dark themes with minimum 7:1 contrast ratio in high-contrast mode, remaining sharp when zoomed up to 400%.
- **Screen Reader Support**: Mathematical formulas and solution accordions include semantic ARIA attributes.
- **Offline Resilient**: All 111 Precalculus lessons are stored in local browser caches for offline study.
