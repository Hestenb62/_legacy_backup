## Grade 8 Mathematics (Pre-Algebra) Complete Curriculum & Interactive Worksheets

The **Grade 8 Mathematics Curriculum** (Level J) provides complete, rigorous coverage of pre-algebra, functions, geometric transformations, and irrational numbers aligned with EngageNY / Eureka Math and national Common Core standards. It is engineered for 8th-grade students, homeschool educators, and classroom teachers seeking structured, distraction-free mathematics mastery.

### Core Architecture & Coverage
Grade 8 features **122 sequential lessons** organized into 7 foundational modules:
- **Module 1: Integer Exponents and Scientific Notation (13 Lessons):** Laws of integer exponents, negative powers, magnitude, and real-world scientific calculations.
- **Module 2: The Concept of Congruence (16 Lessons):** Basic rigid motions (translations, reflections, rotations), congruence of two-dimensional figures, transversals, and Pythagorean Theorem foundations.
- **Module 3: Similarity (14 Lessons):** Dilations, scale factors, coordinate rules, similarity transformations, and angle-angle similarity criteria.
- **Module 4: Linear Equations (31 Lessons):** Writing and solving linear equations in one variable, graphing lines, slope and constant rate of change, and systems of linear equations.
- **Module 5: Examples of Functions from Geometry (11 Lessons):** Function definitions, input-output tables, linear vs. non-linear functions, and volumes of cylinders, cones, and spheres.
- **Module 6: Linear Functions (14 Lessons):** Modeling linear relationships, bivariate numerical data, scatter plots, lines of best fit, and two-way frequency tables.
- **Module 7: Introduction to Irrational Numbers Using Geometry (23 Lessons):** Square and cube roots, decimal expansions of rational and irrational numbers, Pythagorean Theorem proofs, and coordinate distance.

### Interactive Worksheet-First Experience
Every lesson in Grade 8 provides a clean, focused, distraction-free worksheet layout:
- **Direct Worksheet Display:** Pure focus on authentic problem set exercises, eliminating external links and research distractions.
- **Auto-Saving Digital Scratchpads:** Interactive textareas for every problem that persist student work in `localStorage` and synchronize with the cloud.
- **Teacher Solution Keys:** Instant expandable solution accordions displaying step-by-step mathematical reasoning and answers.
- **1-Click Browser Printing:**
  - **Print Blank:** Outputs cleanly formatted, paper-based worksheets with student name lines and ruled handwriting boxes.
  - **Print Solved Work:** Formats typed student notes and solutions into a completed homework packet ready for submission.

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Action Controls
- `Tab` / `Shift + Tab`: Seamlessly navigate through problem cards, action buttons, and digital scratchpad fields.
- `Space` / `Enter`: Expand and collapse teacher solution accordions and toggle print modes.
- `Ctrl + P` (or `Cmd + P`): Direct trigger for high-fidelity print output.
- `Esc`: Dismiss modal overlays and exit ticket assessment runners.

### Accessibility & UDL Compliance
- **Universal MathJax SVG Rendering:** Every mathematical formula and symbol scales dynamically, supports screen magnification up to 400%, and inherits high-contrast foreground color (`currentColor`).
- **WCAG AA/AAA Color Independence:** Meets strict contrast requirements in Light, Midnight Dark, Sepia, and AAA High-Contrast presets.
- **Assistive Technology Optimization:** Every problem card, solution accordion, and input area is equipped with semantic ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label`, `aria-controls`).
- **Offline Resiliency:** Fully cached by the PWA service worker; all 7 modules and interactive practice worksheets remain completely accessible without an internet connection.
