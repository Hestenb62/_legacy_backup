## Grade 3 Mathematics Complete Curriculum & Interactive Worksheets

The **Grade 3 Mathematics Curriculum** (Level E) delivers rigorous, foundational elementary mathematics instruction aligned with EngageNY / Eureka Math and national Common Core standards. Designed for 3rd-grade students, classroom educators, and homeschoolers, this course deepens conceptual understanding through visual models, number bonds, arrays, tape diagrams, and real-world word problems.

### Core Architecture & Coverage
Grade 3 features **152 sequential lessons** organized across 7 comprehensive modules:
- **Module 1: Properties of Multiplication and Division and Solving Problems with Units of 2–5 and 10 (21 Lessons):** Multiplication and division concepts, arrays, commutative and distributive properties, division as an unknown factor problem, and one- and two-step word problems.
- **Module 2: Place Value and Problem Solving with Units of Measure (21 Lessons):** Time measurement to the nearest minute, elapsed time, metric capacity and mass estimation (grams, kilograms, liters), rounding to the nearest ten and hundred, and two- and three-digit addition and subtraction algorithms.
- **Module 3: Multiplication and Division with Units of 0, 1, 6–9, and Multiples of 10 (21 Lessons):** Properties of multiplication and division extended to 6, 7, 8, 9, 0, and 1, analyzing patterns in the multiplication table, multiplying single-digit numbers by multiples of 10, and two-step problem solving.
- **Module 4: Multiplication and Area (16 Lessons):** Foundations of area, tiling rectangular figures, relating area to array multiplication and addition, decomposing rectilinear figures to find total area, and area applications.
- **Module 5: Fractions as Numbers on the Number Line (30 Lessons):** Partitioning shapes into equal fractional parts, unit fractions, fractions on the number line, fraction equivalence using visual models, comparing fractions with like numerators or denominators, and fractions greater than 1.
- **Module 6: Collecting and Displaying Data (9 Lessons):** Generating categorical data, drawing scaled picture graphs and bar graphs, solving one- and two-step problems using graph data, and measuring lengths to the nearest quarter inch to generate line plots.
- **Module 7: Geometry and Measurement Word Problems (34 Lessons):** Solving word problems involving perimeter and unknown side lengths, two-dimensional shape classification (polygons, quadrilaterals, rectangles, rhombuses), perimeter and area relationships, and year-in-review math activities.

### Interactive Worksheet-First Experience
Every lesson in Grade 3 provides a clean, focused, distraction-free worksheet layout:
- **Direct Worksheet Display:** Pure focus on authentic problem set exercises, eliminating external links and research distractions.
- **Auto-Saving Digital Scratchpads:** Interactive textareas for every problem that persist student work in `localStorage` and synchronize with the cloud.
- **Teacher Solution Keys:** Instant expandable solution accordions displaying step-by-step mathematical reasoning and answers.
- **1-Click Browser Printing:**
  - **Print Blank:** Outputs cleanly formatted, paper-based worksheets with student name lines and ruled handwriting boxes.
  - **Print Solved Work:** Formats typed student notes and solutions into a completed homework packet ready for submission.

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Action Controls
- `Tab` / `Shift + Tab`: Seamlessly navigate through problem cards, action buttons, and digital scratchpad fields.
- `Space` / `Enter`: Expand and collapse teacher solution accordions and toggle print modes.
- `Ctrl + P` (or `Cmd + P`): Direct trigger for high-fidelity print output.
- `Esc`: Dismiss modal overlays and exit ticket assessment runners.

### Accessibility & UDL Compliance
- **Universal MathJax SVG Rendering:** Every mathematical formula and symbol scales dynamically, supports screen magnification up to 400%, and inherits high-contrast foreground color (`currentColor`).
- **WCAG AA/AAA Color Independence:** Meets strict contrast requirements in Light, Midnight Dark, Sepia, and AAA High-Contrast presets.
- **Assistive Technology Optimization:** Every problem card, solution accordion, and input area is equipped with semantic ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label`, `aria-controls`).
- **Offline Resiliency:** Fully cached by the PWA service worker; all 7 modules and interactive practice worksheets remain completely accessible without an internet connection.
