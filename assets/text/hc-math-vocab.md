## Mathematics Vocabulary Codex & Review Hub (pages/math-vocab.php)

Master the formal language, symbolic notation, and core definitions of mathematics. The Mathematics Vocabulary Review Hub aggregates every mathematical term, theorem, formula, and conceptual definition from all curriculum lessons into an interactive, accessible review center.

### Quick Access & Navigation
- **Direct Web Endpoint:** Visit `/pages/math-vocab.php` directly from your browser.
- **Top Navigation Menu:** Click **Curriculum ➔ Math Vocabulary Hub** in the header.
- **Quick Links Footer:** Open **Math Vocab Hub** under the platform Quick Links column.
- **Command Palette:** Press `Ctrl + K` and type `vocab` or `math terms` to jump directly.

### Dual Review Modes & Study Superpowers
1. **Codex Grid View:**
   - **Alphabetical Concordance:** Jump quickly between letters A through Z with the sticky alphabetical ribbon.
   - **Multi-Field Live Search:** Search across term names, formal definitions, algebraic formulas, standards, and originating lesson titles in real time.
   - **Domain & Category Filters:** Focus on specific mathematical branches including *Linear & Piecewise Functions*, *Quadratic Functions & Equations*, *Polynomials & Algebraic Expressions*, *Exponential Functions*, *Equations & Inequalities*, *Descriptive Statistics*, and *Number Systems*.
   - **Lesson Backlink Chips:** Click any curriculum lesson chip to jump directly into the source lesson where the concept was taught.

2. **Active Recall 3D Flashcard Studio:**
   - **3D Flip-Card Engine:** Click the card or press **Spacebar** to flip between the front prompt and the back definition/formula.
   - **Keyboard Navigation Hotkeys:**
     - `Space`: Flip the card front/back.
     - `ArrowRight`: Advance to the next flashcard.
     - `ArrowLeft`: Return to the previous flashcard.
     - `S`: Star or unstar the card for targeted review.
   - **Randomized Shuffle:** Click **Shuffle** to randomize the study queue for optimal interleaved retrieval practice.
   - **Self-Assessment Rating:** Mark cards as **Still Learning** or **Mastered** to dynamically organize your study sessions.

3. **Mastery Tracking & Queue Sync:**
   - Filter your cards or grid by **All Words**, **Starred for Review**, **Still Learning**, or **Mastered**.
   - Your study queue automatically saves in `localStorage` and synchronizes across devices with Google Drive auto-sync.

4. **Export & Print Capabilities:**
   - **Export Study Guide:** Download a clean `.txt` summary of your currently filtered terms, definitions, and formulas.
   - **Print Vocabulary Sheet:** Trigger a print-optimized layout formatted specifically for 3-ring binder inserts and physical revision sheets.

### Multimodal Accessibility & UDL Features
- **Auditory Speech Read-Aloud:** Click the volume icon on any card or flashcard to hear natural speech pronunciation of the term and its complete definition.
- **Universal MathJax Typography:** Complex mathematical equations, piecewise cases, exponents, and fractions render in high-resolution, contrast-compliant SVG notation that scales responsively down to 320px screens.
- **Keyboard-First Design:** Complete 100% keyboard operability (`Tab`, `Shift + Tab`, `Enter`, `Space`, `Arrows`, `Esc`) with visible high-contrast focus rings.
- **Screen Reader Compatibility:** Uses semantic headings, ARIA live regions, and descriptive buttons for seamless screen reader navigation.
