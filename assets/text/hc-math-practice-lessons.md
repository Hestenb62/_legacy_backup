## Mathematics Practice Interactive Popups & Problem Sets

The **Mathematics Practice Workbench** (`/student/math-practice.php`) provides learners of all ages with curriculum-aligned lessons, step-by-step worked formulas, and practice check exercises across 6 major mathematical branches and 24 specialized topics:

1. **Algebra**: Basic Algebra, Linear Equations, Quadratic Equations, Systems of Equations
2. **Geometry**: Angles and Lines, Area and Perimeter, Volume and Surface Area, Geometric Proofs
3. **Calculus**: Limits and Continuity, Differentiation, Integration, Applications of Calculus
4. **Statistics**: Descriptive Statistics, Probability Distributions, Hypothesis Testing, Regression Analysis
5. **Number Theory**: Prime Numbers, Divisibility Rules, Modular Arithmetic, Diophantine Equations
6. **Applied Math**: Financial Math, Physics Applications, Engineering Problems, Data Science Challenges

### Core Features & Capabilities

- **Interactive Comprehensive Study Modals**: Clicking any topic pill or the primary "Explore" card button instantly opens a complete instructional terminal:
  - **Master Equations & Formula Vault**: Labeled canonical formulas, general forms, and mathematical theorems typeset via crisp MathJax SVG notation.
  - **Mathematical Visualizations & Coordinate Graphs**: Custom vector diagrams (equilibrium scales, Cartesian coordinate lines, parabolas with vertices, Riemann sums, normal bell curves, factor trees, clock arithmetic dials, truss bridges, and gradient descent paths).
  - **Curated Educational References & Interactive Calculators**: Direct access to Common Core Standards, GeoGebra simulations, Desmos graphing tools, and Hesten's Learning Library textbooks.
  - **Concept Lecture & Subject Breakdown**: Rigorous academic definitions broken down into 3 structural pillars.
  - **Dual-Lexile Readability Toggle**: Instantly switch between *Standard Academic Lexile* and *Basic English (Simplified)* without losing place or context.
  - **Real-World Examples & Worked Solutions**: Practical, step-by-step demonstrations formatted with clear derivation steps and checks.
  - **Rules & Common Pitfalls**: Expert callouts highlighting common misunderstandings, algebraic signs, or common errors.
  - **Interactive Practice Check**: Immediate formative self-assessment questions with instant feedback and explanatory rationale.
- **Universal MathJax SVG Typography**: All algebraic formulas, Greek variables ($\theta, \mu, \sigma$), exponents ($x^2$), square roots, matrices, and calculus integrals ($\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$) render via crisp, scalable, high-contrast SVG that inherits your active theme colors.
- **Real-Time Topic Search & Domain Filters**: Filter math cards on the fly by typing keywords into the search box or clicking domain tabs (Algebra, Geometry, Calculus, Statistics, Number Theory, Applied Math).
- **Deep-Linked URL Hashes**: Every math topic has a persistent URL slug (e.g., `#quadratic-equations`, `#limits-and-continuity`). Copy shareable links directly to jump straight into specific topics.

### Accessibility & Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

- **Multimodal Audio Read-Aloud**: Integrated Text-to-Speech (TTS) via the "Listen" button articulates definitions, examples, and rules.
- **Full Keyboard Navigation**: Complete modal focus trap, `Escape` key dismissal, `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` cycling, and `Enter`/`Space` quiz button activation.
- **Dyscalculia & Sensory Support**: Works with high-contrast themes, OpenDyslexic font toggles, and dyscalculia symbol colorizer.
