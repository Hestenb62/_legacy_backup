## Grade 4 Mathematics Complete Curriculum & Interactive Worksheets

The **Grade 4 Mathematics Curriculum** (Level F) delivers comprehensive, rigorous coverage of elementary mathematics foundations aligned with EngageNY / Eureka Math and national Common Core standards. It is tailored for 4th-grade students, homeschool educators, and classroom teachers seeking structured, distraction-free mathematics mastery.

### Core Architecture & Coverage
Grade 4 features **153 sequential lessons** organized across 7 foundational modules:
- **Module 1: Place Value, Rounding, and Algorithms for Addition and Subtraction (19 Lessons):** Place value of multi-digit whole numbers, comparing and rounding whole numbers, standard addition and subtraction algorithms, and multi-step word problems.
- **Module 2: Unit Conversions and Problem Solving with Metric Measurement (5 Lessons):** Metric unit conversions (km, m, cm, kg, g, L, mL) and multi-step measurement problem solving.
- **Module 3: Multi-Digit Multiplication and Division (38 Lessons):** Multiplicative comparison, multiplication by multiples of 10, multi-digit by single-digit multiplication, division with remainders, reasoning with divisibility, and two-digit multiplication.
- **Module 4: Angle Measure and Plane Figures (16 Lessons):** Lines, rays, angles, angle measurement with protractors, right/acute/obtuse angles, angle addition problem solving, two-dimensional figures, and line symmetry.
- **Module 5: Fraction Equivalence, Ordering, and Operations (41 Lessons):** Decomposition and fraction equivalence, multiplication/division for equivalent fractions, fraction comparison, addition/subtraction with like and unlike denominators, mixed numbers, and multiplying fractions by whole numbers.
- **Module 6: Decimal Fractions (16 Lessons):** Exploration of tenths, tenths and hundredths, decimal comparison on the number line, addition with tenths and hundredths, and money amounts as decimal numbers.
- **Module 7: Exploring Measurement with Multiplication (18 Lessons):** Measurement conversion tables, problem solving with measurement, investigation of measurements expressed as mixed numbers, and year-in-review math project.

### Interactive Worksheet-First Experience
Every lesson in Grade 4 provides a clean, focused, distraction-free worksheet layout:
- **Direct Worksheet Display:** Pure focus on authentic problem set exercises, eliminating external links and research distractions.
- **Auto-Saving Digital Scratchpads:** Interactive textareas for every problem that persist student work in `localStorage` and synchronize with the cloud.
- **Teacher Solution Keys:** Instant expandable solution accordions displaying step-by-step mathematical reasoning and answers.
- **1-Click Browser Printing:**
  - **Print Blank:** Outputs cleanly formatted, paper-based worksheets with student name lines and ruled handwriting boxes.
  - **Print Solved Work:** Formats typed student notes and solutions into a completed homework packet ready for submission.

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Action Controls
- `Tab` / `Shift + Tab`: Seamlessly navigate through problem cards, action buttons, and digital scratchpad fields.
- `Space` / `Enter`: Expand and collapse teacher solution accordions and toggle print modes.
- `Ctrl + P` (or `Cmd + P`): Direct trigger for high-fidelity print output.
- `Esc`: Dismiss modal overlays and exit ticket assessment runners.

### Accessibility & UDL Compliance
- **Universal MathJax SVG Rendering:** Every mathematical formula and symbol scales dynamically, supports screen magnification up to 400%, and inherits high-contrast foreground color (`currentColor`).
- **WCAG AA/AAA Color Independence:** Meets strict contrast requirements in Light, Midnight Dark, Sepia, and AAA High-Contrast presets.
- **Assistive Technology Optimization:** Every problem card, solution accordion, and input area is equipped with semantic ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label`, `aria-controls`).
- **Offline Resiliency:** Fully cached by the PWA service worker; all 7 modules and interactive practice worksheets remain completely accessible without an internet connection.
