## Digital Library Master Punctuation Guide & Research Repositories

The **Digital Library Master Punctuation Guide** (`/library/index.php?pages=punctuation`) is an authoritative, interactive academic repository covering the rules, syntactic functions, style variances, contrasting exemplars, and historical evolution of English punctuation. Built with an extensible modular architecture, it serves middle school, high school, and collegiate scholars preparing for advanced writing and standardized assessments (CCSS, SAT, ACT, AP).

### 16 Core Punctuation Marks Covered

1. **Terminal Punctuation**: Period (Full Stop), Question Mark, Exclamation Mark
2. **Pausing & Joining**: Comma, Semicolon, Colon, Hyphen
3. **Dashes & Spans**: En Dash (–), Em Dash (—)
4. **Enclosing & Framing**: Apostrophe, Quotation Marks, Parentheses, Square Brackets
5. **Omission & Specialized**: Ellipsis, Slash (Virgule), Interrobang (‽)

### Key Features & Scholar Tools

- **Extensible Research Page Routing**: Access via `/library/index.php?pages=punctuation` (or any configured subject page slug), powered by structured external JSON datasets in `library/assets/pages/`.
- **Comprehensive Mark Directories**:
  - **Mark Symbol & Unicode Box**: Clear typographic glyph display with single-click "Copy Glyph" functionality.
  - **Syntactic Purpose & Role**: Precise linguistic definition and clause function.
  - **Numbered Canonical Rules**: Rigorous guidelines accompanied by syntactic rationales and Common Core / style citations.
  - **Contrasting Exemplars Grid**: Side-by-side comparison of **Correct Academic Usage** vs. **Common Misuse / Traps** (comma splices, dangling modifiers, colon gatekeeper violations, British vs. American quotation nesting) with in-depth diagnostic explanations.
  - **Historical Evolution & Etymology**: Tracing mark origins from Aristophanes of Byzantium (200 BCE) and Aldus Manutius (1494) to modern Unicode standards.
  - **Pro-Tips & Common Pitfalls**: Practical mnemonics and editorial conventions.
- **Academic Style Guide Divergence Matrix**: Side-by-side comparison of Chicago Manual of Style (CMOS), MLA 9th, APA 7th, and AP Style conventions across contentious debates (Oxford comma, em dash spacing, post-colon capitalization, and quotation placement).
- **High-Density Quick-Reference Cheat Sheet**: Clean, scannable table outlining primary functions, mandatory practices, and prohibited errors for all 16 marks.
- **Deep-Link Hash Navigation**: Smooth scrolling and visual card pulsing when accessing direct anchors (e.g., `/library/index.php?pages=punctuation#comma`, `#semicolon`, `#cheat-sheet`).
- **Research Export Tools**: Instant citation generator (MLA, APA, Chicago) and dedicated print stylesheet (`@media print`) for printable study sheets.

### Accessibility & Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

- **Multimodal Audio Read-Aloud**: Dedicated "Listen" button on each mark card leveraging Web Speech API synthesis for auditory learning.
- **Full Keyboard Operability**: Complete keyboard navigation across category tabs (`Tab`, `Enter`, `Space`), search inputs, action buttons, and jump links.
- **WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA & AAA Contrast**: Guaranteed contrast ratios (≥4.5:1 AA, ≥7:1 AAA high contrast), explicit `:focus-visible` focus rings, and seamless styling across Light, Dark, Midnight, and Sepia themes.
- **Mobile Responsive & Offline Resilient**: Reflows down to 320px viewport without horizontal clipping; cached offline by service workers.
