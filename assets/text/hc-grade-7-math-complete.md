## Grade 7 Mathematics: Complete Curriculum Guide

### Executive Overview
Grade 7 Mathematics on Hesten's Learning represents a complete 139-lesson curriculum aligned with the New York State Common Core Learning Standards and EngageNY / Eureka Math (A Story of Ratios). Covering 6 comprehensive modules, the curriculum bridges concrete elementary arithmetic with abstract algebraic, geometric, and statistical thinking.

### 6-Module Scope & Sequence (139 Lessons)
* **Module 1: Ratios and Proportional Relationships (22 Lessons):** Proportional vs. non-proportional relationships, unit rates as constants of proportionality ($y = kx$), ratios involving fractions, multi-step ratio problems, and scale drawings.
* **Module 2: Rational Numbers (23 Lessons):** Positive and negative integer arithmetic, additive inverses, absolute difference on a number line, long division fraction-to-decimal conversions, signed multiplication/division, and multi-step algebraic word problems.
* **Module 3: Expressions and Equations (26 Lessons):** Linear expressions, distributive property, multi-step equations and inequalities with signed numbers, angle relationships (supplementary, complementary, vertical), circle circumference and area ($C = 2\pi r$, $A = \pi r^2$), surface area, and volume of right prisms.
* **Module 4: Percent and Proportional Relationships (18 Lessons):** Percent of a quantity, markups, markdowns, percent error, simple interest ($I = Prt$), sales tax, gratuities, commissions, scale drawings with percent, and population estimation.
* **Module 5: Statistics and Probability (23 Lessons):** Chance experiments, theoretical vs. experimental probability, tree diagrams, compound events, simulation models, random sampling techniques, sampling variability, and comparing two populations.
* **Module 6: Geometry (27 Lessons):** Unknown angles, conditions for constructing unique triangles, cross-sections and slices of 3D solids, composite area, surface area, and volume of right triangular/rectangular prisms.

### Core Student Capabilities & Study Tools
* **Authentic Embedded Problem Sets:** Distraction-free worksheets integrated into each lesson page with step-by-step worked teacher keys.
* **Universal MathJax SVG Typography:** Pristine rendering of mathematical symbols, fractions, radicals, exponents, and geometry angles.
* **Interactive Digital Working Areas:** Auto-saving textareas (`localStorage`) allowing scholars to record steps and solutions without losing work.
* **1-Click Printing:** `printProblemSet()` formats clean worksheets for offline classroom practice.

### Accessibility & UDL Compliance
* **Multimodal Representation:** High-contrast focus outlines, screen-reader semantic landmarks, and adjustable font sizing.
* **100% Keyboard Operability:** All accordions, tabs, and worksheet inputs are fully navigable via `Tab`, `Shift+Tab`, `Enter`, and `Space`.
* **Low-Anxiety & Untimed Practice:** Untimed options and low-stress self-check tools supporting neurodivergent learners.
