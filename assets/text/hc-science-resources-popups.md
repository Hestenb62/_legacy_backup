## Science Resources Interactive Study Modals & Scientific Principle Vaults

The **Science Resources Terminal** provides students and educators with comprehensive, curriculum-aligned scientific inquiry lessons, mathematical and empirical formulas, system diagrams, and formative practice checks across 6 fundamental scientific disciplines and 24 specialized topics:

1. **Biology Resources**: Cell Biology, Genetics & Heredity, Ecology & Environment, Human Body Systems
2. **Chemistry Resources**: Periodic Table, Chemical Reactions, Acids and Bases, Organic Chemistry Basics
3. **Physics Resources**: Mechanics, Electricity & Magnetism, Waves & Optics, Thermodynamics
4. **Earth Science Resources**: Geology & Rocks, Weather & Climate, Oceanography, Astronomy & Space
5. **Science Experiments**: Biology Labs, Chemistry Demos, Physics Simulations, At-Home Experiments
6. **Science Articles & News**: Latest Discoveries, Environmental Science News, Space Exploration Articles, Health & Medicine Updates

### Core Features & Capabilities

- **Interactive Comprehensive Study Modals**: Clicking any science topic card or badge launches a rich, accessible learning modal:
  - **Scientific Laws & Mathematical Principles Vault**: Labeled equations, stoichiometric ratios, kinematics formulas, and thermodynamic laws typeset via crisp MathJax SVG notation.
  - **Scientific Models & Systemic Diagrams**: Handcrafted vector graphics (eukaryotic cell architecture, Punnett squares, Bohr electron models, refraction ray paths, heat engines, the rock cycle, atmospheric circulation cells, and mRNA vaccine immunology).
  - **Curated Educational References & Academic Guides**: Direct deep links to Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS), NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, NOAA, USGS, NCBI PubMed, American Chemical Society, and PhET Interactive Simulations.
  - **Concept Lecture & Subject Breakdown**: Rigorous scientific definitions broken down into 3 foundational core pillars.
  - **Dual-Lexile Readability Toggle**: Instantly switch between *Standard Academic Lexile* and *Basic English (Simplified)* without losing context.
  - **Real-World Quantitative Examples**: Applied calculations and scientific observations grounded in empirical data.
  - **Pro-Tips & Lab Techniques**: Essential laboratory practices, mnemonic rules, and safety principles (e.g. meniscus reading, fair testing variables, microscope lens progression).
  - **Formative Practice Checks**: Immediate self-assessment questions with instant feedback and explanatory breakdowns.
- **Universal MathJax SVG Typography**: All chemical formulas, reaction arrows, physical constants, and equations render with high contrast, responsive scaling, and theme inheritance across light and dark modes.
- **Real-Time Topic Search & Domain Filters**: Search and filter science resources dynamically by typing keywords or clicking domain pills.

### Accessibility & Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

- **Multimodal Audio Read-Aloud**: Integrated Text-to-Speech (TTS) via the "Listen" button articulates definitions, examples, and scientific breakdowns.
- **Full Keyboard Navigation**: Complete modal focus trap, `Escape` key dismissal, `Tab` cycling, and `Enter`/`Space` quiz choice selection.
- **Dyslexia & Sensory Accommodations**: Full compatibility with OpenDyslexic font toggles, Irlen color overlays, bionic reading, and AAA high-contrast modes.
- **Accessible Vector Diagrams**: Every diagram is a native responsive SVG equipped with `role="img"`, descriptive `aria-label`, high color contrast, and scalable viewboxes.
