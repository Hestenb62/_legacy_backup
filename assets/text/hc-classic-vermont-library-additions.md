### Collection Highlights

#### 1. Classic Literature & Sacred Texts
- **Wuthering Heights (Emily Brontë, eBook #768):**
  - 34 complete chapters tracing the intense, fateful relationship of Heathcliff and Catherine across Wuthering Heights and Thrushcross Grange.
  - Formatted with single-column natural reading typography and chapter time estimates.
- **The King James Bible (Authorized Version 1611, eBook #10):**
  - All 66 canonical books formatted as discrete navigable chapters: 39 Old Testament books (Genesis through Malachi) and 27 New Testament books (Matthew through Revelation).
  - High-speed drawer navigation allows instant jumping across books.

#### 2. Vermont State Park & Forest Geology Series (Earth Science)
- **The Geology of Button Bay State Park (Harry W. Dodge, eBook #61230):**
  - Examines Lake Champlain shorelines, Champlain Sea clay strata, and round calcareous concretions ("button stones").
- **The Geology of Calvin Coolidge State Forest (Harry W. Dodge, eBook #61400):**
  - Field guide to Green Mountain metamorphic rocks, gold panning along Buffalo Brook in Plymouth, and talc/garnet quarrying.
- **The Geology of Darling State Park (Harry W. Dodge, eBook #60730):**
  - Explores Burke Mountain granites, hornfels contact metamorphism, and glacial summits in the Northeast Kingdom.
- **The Geology of D.A.R., Mt. Philo, and Sand Bar State Parks (Harry W. Dodge, eBook #60989):**
  - Studies Champlain Valley paleontology, the famous Champlain Thrust fault cliff at Mt. Philo, and deltaic sand bars.
- **The Geology of Groton State Forest (Robert A. Christman, eBook #60710):**
  - Details the Knox Mountain granite pluton, pegmatites, and roche moutonnée landforms.
- **The Geology of Mt. Mansfield State Forest (Robert A. Christman, eBook #62053):**
  - Surveys the metamorphic schists of Vermont's highest summit, alpine periglacial phenomena, and the glacial notch at Smugglers Notch.

#### 3. Vermont Early Republic & Civil War History (US History)
- **The Green Mountain Boys : or, Vermont and the New York land jobbers (Philip H. Smith, eBook #74334):**
  - 13 chapters detailing Ethan Allen, the New Hampshire Grants dispute, the capture of Fort Ticonderoga, and the independent Republic of Vermont.
- **Vermont: A Study of Independence (Rowland Evans Robinson, eBook #35573):**
  - 25 chapters tracing Vermont from indigenous paths and the French and Indian Wars through statehood, 1812, and the Civil War.
- **Vermont Riflemen in the War for the Union, 1861 to 1865 (Lt. Col. William Y. W. Ripley, eBook #35088):**
  - 8 chapters recounting Company F, 1st U.S. Sharpshooters (Berdan's Sharpshooters), from Rutland target trials to the Wilderness, Cold Harbor, and Petersburg.

### Universal Design for Learning (UDL) & Accessibility
- **Single-Column Natural Flow:** Standardized reading layout optimized for distraction-free comprehension without forced multi-column breaks.
- **Audio & Speech:** Integrated Web Speech API Text-to-Speech narration with word highlighting.
- **Visual Accommodations:** Dynamic toggle between standard typefaces, OpenDyslexic, and Atkinson Hyperlegible; custom line-spacing, margins, and contrast themes (Light, Sepia, Solarized Dark, Midnight).
- **Responsive Illustrations:** High-resolution geological diagrams, maps, and engravings rendered with responsive containers and lazy loading.
