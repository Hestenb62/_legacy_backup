## Kindergarten Mathematics Complete Curriculum & Interactive Worksheets

The **Kindergarten Mathematics Curriculum** (Level B) introduces young scholars to core mathematical concepts, number representations, counting, comparing, basic addition and subtraction, and 2D/3D shapes aligned with EngageNY / Eureka Math and national Common Core standards.

### Core Architecture & Coverage
Kindergarten features **152 sequential lessons** organized across 6 engaging modules:
- **Module 1: Numbers to 10 (37 Lessons):** Classifying and sorting objects, attributes of groups, numbers 1–5 (counting, matching, writing numerals), numbers 6–10 (scattered configurations, linear counting, ten-frame representations), and one more/one less relationships.
- **Module 2: Two-Dimensional and Three-Dimensional Shapes (10 Lessons):** Identifying and describing 2D shapes (circles, triangles, squares, rectangles, hexagons), positional words (above, below, next to), and 3D solid shapes (cubes, cones, cylinders, spheres).
- **Module 3: Comparison of Length, Weight, Capacity, and Numbers to 10 (32 Lessons):** Direct comparison of length and height, comparing weights with balance scales, volume and capacity, and comparing numbers using "greater than", "less than", and "equal to".
- **Module 4: Number Pairs, Addition and Subtraction to 10 (41 Lessons):** Number bonds, composing and decomposing numbers to 5 and to 10, addition and subtraction stories with manipulatives and pictures, and formal algebraic expressions ($+$, $-$, $=$).
- **Module 5: Numbers 10–20 and Counting to 100 (24 Lessons):** Teen numbers as "10 ones and some more ones", hide zero cards, counting by tens to 100, and writing numerals 10 through 20.
- **Module 6: Analyzing, Comparing, and Composing Shapes (8 Lessons):** Building and composing shapes from smaller components, real-world geometric structures, ordinal numbers, and culminating kindergarten mathematical celebrations.

### Interactive Worksheet-First Experience
Every lesson in Kindergarten provides a clean, focused, distraction-free worksheet layout:
- **Direct Worksheet Display:** Pure focus on authentic problem set exercises, eliminating external links and research distractions.
- **Auto-Saving Digital Scratchpads:** Interactive textareas for every problem that persist student work in `localStorage` and synchronize with the cloud.
- **Teacher Solution Keys:** Instant expandable solution accordions displaying step-by-step mathematical reasoning and answers.
- **1-Click Browser Printing:**
  - **Print Blank:** Outputs cleanly formatted, paper-based worksheets with student name lines and ruled handwriting boxes.
  - **Print Solved Work:** Formats typed student notes and solutions into a completed homework packet ready for submission.

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Action Controls
- `Tab` / `Shift + Tab`: Seamlessly navigate through problem cards, action buttons, and digital scratchpad fields.
- `Space` / `Enter`: Expand and collapse teacher solution accordions and toggle print modes.
- `Ctrl + P` (or `Cmd + P`): Direct trigger for high-fidelity print output.
- `Esc`: Dismiss modal overlays and exit ticket assessment runners.

### Accessibility & UDL Compliance
- **Universal MathJax SVG Rendering:** Every mathematical formula and symbol scales dynamically, supports screen magnification up to 400%, and inherits high-contrast foreground color (`currentColor`).
- **WCAG AA/AAA Color Independence:** Meets strict contrast requirements in Light, Midnight Dark, Sepia, and AAA High-Contrast presets.
- **Assistive Technology Optimization:** Every problem card, solution accordion, and input area is equipped with semantic ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label`, `aria-controls`).
- **Offline Resiliency:** Fully cached by the PWA service worker; all 6 modules and interactive practice worksheets remain completely accessible without an internet connection.
