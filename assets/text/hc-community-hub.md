## Community Hub & Resources Repository

Connect with homeschool cooperatives, educator exchanges, and special education advocacy coalitions across the globe. The Community Hub (`/pages/community.php`) is an open collaborative repository connecting families, teachers, and neurodiversity advocates to exchange resources, access state legal guides, and directly shape the development roadmap of Hesten's Learning.

### Core Superpowers & Features
- **Curated Community Directory:** Filter and discover vetted organizations across 4 key categories:
  - **Homeschool & Co-ops:** Secular homeschool directories, state legal compliance guidelines, curriculum swaps, and local meetup networks.
  - **Educator Exchanges:** Open Educational Resources (OER) Commons, MTSS small-group intervention rubrics, and peer-reviewed lesson plan collaboratives.
  - **Special Education & Neurodiversity:** Understood.org learning difference guides, International Dyslexia Association structured literacy toolkits, dyscalculia math frameworks, and cerebral palsy family toolkits (featuring accessible `(i)` provider disclosures highlighting law firm advocacy, assistive technology, physical/occupational therapies, and Section 504 / IEP school accommodations).
  - **STEAM & Learning Circles:** National Association of Math Circles, Science Olympiad maker spaces, and public library youth creative writing guilds.
- **Live Search & Filter Engine:** Instantly search through community groups, organizations, and resource guides with real-time keyword matching and category filter pills.
- **Two-Way Community Feedback Form:** Submit suggestions, report accessibility barriers, or recommend curriculum enhancements with learning role attribution (Parent, Teacher, Student, Advocate) and offline-first local storage backup (`localStorage`).
- **Interactive Community Roadmap Upvote Board:** Upvote community-submitted feature requests (such as high school science lab sheets, virtual abacus manipulatives, and audio speed controllers) with live, synchronized counters.

### Quick Actions & Navigation
- **Direct Route:** Navigate directly to `/pages/community.php` or jump from the **Resources** header mega-menu, **Support** footer column, **Parent Hub** (`pages/parents.php`), or **Educator Suite** (`pages/teachers.php`).
- **Search Shortcuts:** Press `Tab` to navigate to the live search bar; press `Esc` or click the clear button to reset queries instantly.
- **Upvote Toggling:** Press `Enter` or `Space` on any upvote button to register or withdraw your support for a feature request.

### Universal Accessibility & UDL
- **Screen Reader Announcements:** Upvote count updates and directory search results dynamically announce via `aria-live="polite"` regions.
- **Full Keyboard Operability:** 100% accessible via `Tab`, `Shift+Tab`, `Enter`, and `Space` with unmistakable `:focus-visible` high-contrast focus rings.
- **WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA & AAA Compliance:** Optimized contrast across Light, Dark, and High-Contrast modes with responsive reflow from 320px mobile viewports to ultra-wide displays.
- **Offline Resiliency & Zero Tracking:** Operates offline-first; feedback submissions and upvote states persist securely to local browser storage without third-party tracking or advertising cookies.
