## Grade 9 ELA Module 1: Reading Closely and Writing to Learn

The **Grade 9 English Language Arts (ELA) Module 1 Curriculum** (Level K) delivers rigorous, literature-centered secondary instruction aligned with EngageNY / Core Knowledge and national Common Core State Standards (`RL.9-10.1`, `RL.9-10.4`, `W.9-10.2`, `L.9-10.4`). Tailored for 9th-grade high school scholars, homeschool educators, and English literature classrooms, this course develops disciplined close reading habits, deep text-dependent inquiry, vocabulary fluency, and evidence-based analytical writing.

### Core Architecture & Literature Units
Grade 9 Module 1 features **48 sequential lessons** organized across three foundational literature units:
- **Unit 1: Reading Literature — *St. Lucy’s Home for Girls Raised by Wolves* (17 Lessons):**
  - Close reading of Karen Russell's acclaimed allegorical short story.
  - Tracking the five stages of lycanthropic culture shock, epigraph analysis, narrator perspective (Claudette vs. Mirabella and Jeanette), and the tension between primal wilderness and institutional civilization.
  - Directly linked to the standalone digital reading in the library: `/library/?page=st-lucys-home`.
- **Unit 2: Literary & Informational Dialogue — *Letters to a Young Poet* (11 Lessons):**
  - Analysis of Rainer Maria Rilke's foundational "Letter One" (Paris, 1903).
  - Examining authorial voice, philosophical advice on solitude, the necessity of artistic creation ("Must I write?"), and thematic synthesis with David Mitchell's *Black Swan Green*.
  - Directly linked to the standalone digital reading in the library: `/library/?page=letters-to-a-young-poet`.
- **Unit 3: Classical Drama & Poetic Structure — *The Tragedy of Romeo and Juliet* (20 Lessons):**
  - Detailed exploration of William Shakespeare's immortal drama (Act 1 Prologue through Act 3).
  - Deconstructing iambic pentameter, sonnet structures, fatalism ("star-crossed lovers"), dramatic irony, puns, oxymorons, and central conflicts between ancient feud and youthful passion.
  - Directly linked to the standalone digital reading in the library: `/library/?page=romeo-and-juliet-module-1`.

### Dedicated Library Reading Section Integration
In accordance with user preferences, readings are embedded as standalone, full-length reading article pages in the library—not cataloged as books on shelves:
- **Distraction-Free Reading:** Dedicated reading URLs (`/library/?page=st-lucys-home`, `/library/?page=letters-to-a-young-poet`, `/library/?page=romeo-and-juliet-module-1`).
- **Numbered Paragraph References:** Every paragraph and stanza possesses a permanent anchor ID (`#p1`, `#p2`, etc.) allowing direct cross-referencing between lesson worksheets and texts.
- **Reading Assistance Tools:** Built-in Web Speech Synthesis read-aloud, font size scaling, OpenDyslexic font toggles, and 1-click text printing.
- **Direct Lesson Bridges:** Every ELA lesson features a prominent banner button to launch the full reading in the library in a new tab.

### Interactive Worksheet-First Experience
Every lesson provides an authentic, distraction-free worksheet layout:
- **Text-Dependent Analysis Problem Sets:** Questions extracted directly from EngageNY close-reading activities with auto-saving student textareas (`localStorage`).
- **Synthesizing Quick Writes:** Cumulative analytical writing prompts evaluated using the Short Response Rubric (Inferences/Claims, Analysis, Evidence, and Conventions).
- **Teacher Solution Keys:** Collapsible solution accordions revealing worked analytical models, paragraph citations, and exemplar student responses.
- **1-Click Browser Printing:**
  - **Print Blank Worksheet:** Paper-ready reading questions and lined response spaces for traditional writing.
  - **Print Solved Work:** Formats student digital notes and essays into an organized homework submission packet.

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Action Controls
- `Tab` / `Shift + Tab`: Seamlessly navigate through reading buttons, vocabulary cards, question prompts, and digital working textareas.
- `Space` / `Enter`: Expand and collapse teacher solution keys and trigger audio read-aloud.
- `Ctrl + P` (or `Cmd + P`): Direct trigger for high-fidelity print output.
- `Esc`: Dismiss modal overlays and exit ticket assessment runners.

### Accessibility & UDL Compliance
- **WCAG AA/AAA Color Independence:** Fully compliant contrast across Light, Midnight Dark, Sepia, and AAA High-Contrast presets.
- **Multimodal Design (UDL):** Visual text display, text-to-speech read-aloud, digital typing workspaces, and printable worksheets cater to diverse student learning modalities.
- **Offline Resiliency:** Fully cached by the PWA service worker; all 48 lessons and digital library readings remain accessible without an internet connection.
