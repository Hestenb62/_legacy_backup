## Grade 10 Geometry Curriculum & Master 117-Lesson Buildout

The Grade 10 Geometry curriculum represents a comprehensive, standards-aligned high school geometry course encompassing 117 interactive lessons across five core instructional modules. Derived directly from authentic EngageNY / Eureka Math source materials, every lesson features rigorous deductive proofs, Euclidean compass-and-straightedge constructions, worked teacher examples with MathJax LaTeX typography, embedded official Problem Set worksheets with expandable full solution keys, and verbatim Exit Ticket assessments.

### Curriculum Structure & Module Inventory
- **Module 1: Congruence, Proof, and Constructions (Lessons 1–34)**:
  - *Topics A–B*: Basic constructions (equilateral triangles, angle bisectors, perpendicular bisectors), points of concurrency (incenter, circumcenter, orthocenter, centroid), and unknown angle proofs with transversals.
  - *Topics C–E*: Transformations as functions of the plane (translations, rotations, reflections, line symmetry), rigid motions as distance-preserving isometries, triangle congruence criteria (SAS, ASA, SSS, AAS, HL), and formal parallelogram proofs.
  - *Topics F–G*: Advanced constructions (the nine-point circle) and axiomatic systems.
- **Module 2: Similarity, Proof, and Trigonometry (Lessons 1–34)**:
  - *Topics A–C*: Scale drawings using ratio and parallel methods, triangle side-splitter theorem, properties of dilations, similarity transformations, and similarity criteria (AA, SAS, SSS).
  - *Topics D–E*: Right triangle similarity, Pythagorean theorem proofs, definition of trigonometric ratios (sine, cosine, tangent), complementary angle relationships, and law of sines and cosines applications.
- **Module 3: Extending to Three Dimensions (Lessons 1–13)**:
  - *Topics A–B*: Area of polygonal regions and circular disks, Cavalieri's principle for area and volume, scaling principles in 3D, and volume derivations for prisms, cylinders, pyramids, cones, and spheres.
- **Module 4: Connecting Algebra and Geometry Through Coordinates (Lessons 1–15)**:
  - *Topics A–D*: Coordinate plane modeling, perpendicular and parallel slope criteria ($m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1$), distance formula applications, Green's-type polygon area calculations, and dividing segments into proportional ratios.
- **Module 5: Circles With and Without Coordinates (Lessons 1–21)**:
  - *Topics A–C*: Thales' theorem, central and inscribed angle relationships, arc lengths and radian measure, sector areas, secant and tangent line intersections, and interior/exterior angle theorems.
  - *Topics D–E*: Derivation of circle equations $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$, completing the square to find center and radius, tangent line equations, cyclic quadrilaterals, and Ptolemy's theorem.

### Interactive Features & Study Tools
- **Embedded Problem Set Worksheets**: Every lesson includes full student exercise sets paired with step-by-step teacher solutions, eliminating the need for external PDF downloads.
- **1-Click Printing**: Print dedicated student worksheets and homework practice via the built-in `printProblemSet()` action bar.
- **Step-by-Step Guided Classwork**: Clear visual and algebraic steps for complex geometric constructions and two-column deductive proofs with common pitfall warnings.
- **Verbatim Exit Ticket Rubrics**: Authentic daily mastery checks with official scoring criteria.
- **Dynamic MathJax Typography**: All geometric symbols ($\cong$, $\sim$, $\angle$, $\triangle$, $\perp$, $\parallel$, $\sqrt{}$, fractions) render as responsive vector SVGs inheriting current theme colors.

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Action Controls
- `Tab` / `Shift+Tab`: Navigate sequentially through lesson sections, proof steps, problem set accordions, and action buttons.
- `Enter` or `Space`: Expand/collapse teacher solution keys or activate interactive tabs.
- `Alt + P`: Print the current lesson problem set worksheet.
- `Alt + B`: Bookmark the current geometry skill or standard to your personal study dashboard.
- `Esc`: Close open modal overlays or dismiss standard notification banners.

### Accessibility & Universal Design for Learning (UDL)
- **High Contrast Focus & Vector Rendering**: All geometric equations, theorems, and diagrams render crisply with high contrast (≥7:1 in high contrast mode), remaining perfectly legible when zoomed up to 400%.
- **Screen Reader Announcements**: Mathematical proofs, angle relationships, and solution toggles feature semantic ARIA markup (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label`, `role="region"`).
- **Offline First**: All 117 Grade 10 lessons are cached in client storage for seamless offline access through the platform service worker.
