## Printable Student Progress & IEP Summary Portfolios

Generate formal, presentation-ready academic portfolios and accommodation reports! Accessible from both the Teacher Dashboard (`/pages/teachers.php`) and the Parent Hub (`/pages/parents.php`), this tool exports comprehensive IEP & 504 summaries for school committees, conferences, and student records.

### How to Generate Reports
- **From Teacher Dashboard:** Open any student's Diagnostic Dossier and click the **"Print IEP & Progress Dossier"** button.
- **From Parent Hub:** Under Essential Tools, click the **"Official Progress & IEP Report"** button to open the document generator.

### What is Included in the Portfolio
- **Official Branding:** Clean, standardized header featuring `hestena62.com • 100% Free Open Educational Platform`.
- **Student Metadata:** Full student name, academic grade, evaluation date, and document classification ID.
- **Academic Mastery Distribution:** Standardized counts of Mastered ($\ge 80\%$), Proficient ($60\% - 79\%$), and Developing ($< 60\%$) competencies across Math, ELA, Science, and Social Studies.
- **Active UDL & 504 Accommodations:** Itemized checklist of active accommodations (e.g. untimed mode, OpenDyslexic font, sensory retreat presets, high contrast).
- **Standards Mastery Details:** Complete table of evaluated CCSS standards with scores, proficiency status, and diagnostic educator notes.
- **Official Signatures Section:** Designated signature and date verification blocks for General Educator, Special Education Specialist, and Parent / Guardian.

### Clean Print Technology
- **Isolated Iframe Generation:** Prints only the clean, multi-page dossier document without capturing surrounding web browser navigation bars, sidebars, or modal backdrops.
- **Ink-Friendly Typography:** Styled with high-contrast, black-and-white print CSS optimized for physical laser printers and clean PDF saves.
