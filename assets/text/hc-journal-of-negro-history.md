### Volume-by-Volume Academic Highlights

#### Volume 1 (1916, eBook #13642 — 36 Chapters)
- **Inaugural Issue:** The launching volume edited by Dr. Carter G. Woodson establishing Black historical scholarship.
- **Key Monographs:**
  - *The Negroes of Cincinnati Prior to the Civil War* (Carter G. Woodson)
  - *The Story of Maria Louise Moore and Fannie M. Richards*
  - *The Mind of the African Negro as Reflected in His Proverbs*
  - *What the Negro Was Thinking During the Eighteenth Century*
  - *The Negro Soldier in the American Revolution*
  - *Freedom and Slavery in Appalachian America*
  - *Travelers' Impressions of Slavery in America from 1750 to 1800*

#### Volume 2 (1917, eBook #20752 — 33 Chapters)
- **Slavery & Intellectual History:**
  - *Slavery and the Slave Trade in Africa*
  - *The Negro in the Field of Invention*
  - *Anthony Benezet and John Woolman's Abolitionist Ministries*
  - *People of Color in Louisiana*
  - *The Formation of the American Colonization Society*
  - *Three Negro Poets: Horton, Mrs. Harper, and Whitman*
  - *First Biennial Meeting of the Association for the Study of Negro Life and History*

#### Volume 3 (1918, eBook #20906 — 32 Chapters)
- **Biographies & Pioneer Institutions:**
  - *The Story of Josiah Henson* (the real-life inspiration for Uncle Tom's Cabin)
  - *Palmares: The Negro Numantia* (runaway slave republic in Brazil)
  - *Benjamin Banneker, the Negro Mathematician and Astronomer*
  - *George Liele and Andrew Bryan, Pioneer Negro Baptist Preachers*
  - *Fifty Years of Howard University*
  - *Slavery in Kentucky & Miscegenation Beginnings*

#### Volume 4 (1919, eBook #21093 — 27 Chapters)
- **Law, Colonization & Civil War:**
  - *Primitive Law and the Negro*
  - *Lincoln's Plan for Colonizing the Emancipated Negroes*
  - *Lemuel Haynes (Revolutionary Minuteman & Pastor)*
  - *The Employment of Negroes as Soldiers in the Confederate Army*
  - *Negro Life and History in Our Schools*
  - *Labor Conditions in Jamaica Prior to 1917*

#### Volume 5 (1920, eBook #23200 — 37 Chapters)
- **Education, Migration & Insurrection:**
  - *The Negro in Education & Development of the Missouri Public School System*
  - *The Negro Migration to Canada After the Fugitive Slave Act*
  - *Relations of Negroes and Indians in Massachusetts*
  - *The Aftermath of Nat Turner's Insurrection*
  - *Reconstruction Legislators & Congressmen*
  - *Diplomatic Relations Bearing on Return of Slaves (1783–1828)*

#### Volume 6 (1921, eBook #22149 — 35 Chapters)
- **Constitutional Rights & Global Pan-Africanism:**
  - *Fifty Years of Negro Citizenship as Qualified by the United States Supreme Court* (Dr. Woodson's masterwork on 14th Amendment jurisprudence, locomotion rights, and civil equity)
  - *Canadian Negroes and the John Brown Raid*
  - *Economic Condition of Negroes of New York Prior to 1861*
  - *The Material Culture of Ancient Nigeria*
  - *The Great Negro Migration of 1916–1918 (Chapters I–X)*

#### Volume 7 (1922, eBook #24484 — 32 Chapters)
- **Churches, Congressmen & Comparative Slavery:**
  - *Slave Society on the Southern Plantation*
  - *The Evolution of the Negro Baptist Church*
  - *Early Negro Education in West Virginia*
  - *Negro Congressmen a Generation After & Biographical Studies of Hiram Revels and Blanche K. Bruce*
  - *Brazilian and United States Slavery Compared*
  - *Lott Cary: The Colonizing Missionary*
