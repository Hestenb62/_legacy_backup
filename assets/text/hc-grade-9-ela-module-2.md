## Grade 9 ELA Module 2: Understanding Perspectives and Writing to Convince

The **Grade 9 English Language Arts (ELA) Module 2 Curriculum** (Level K) deepens high school students' ability to analyze authorial perspective, unreliable narration, classical tragic irony, and rhetorical arguments aligned with EngageNY / Core Knowledge and Common Core State Standards (`RL.9-10.1`, `RL.9-10.2`, `RL.9-10.5`, `RI.9-10.2`, `RI.9-10.5`, `W.9-10.2`).

### Core Architecture & Literature Units
Grade 9 Module 2 features **46 sequential lessons** organized across three profound units:
- **Unit 1: Literary Perspective — *The Tell-Tale Heart* & *I felt a Funeral, in my Brain* (13 Lessons):**
  - Close reading of Edgar Allan Poe’s masterwork of psychological terror and unreliable narration.
  - Tracking narrative pacing, acute sensory perception, obsessive guilt, and structural repetition.
  - Comparative analysis with Emily Dickinson’s Poem 280 (*I felt a Funeral, in my Brain*), exploring mental unraveling through sensory and auditory funeral metaphors.
  - Dedicated standalone library reading pages: `/library/?page=the-tell-tale-heart` and `/library/?page=i-felt-a-funeral-in-my-brain`.
- **Unit 2: Classical Tragedy & Dramatic Irony — *Oedipus the King* (20 Lessons):**
  - In-depth study of Sophocles’ classic Greek tragedy.
  - Analyzing dramatic irony, hubris, divine oracles vs. human agency, the blind prophet Tiresias, and the tragic catastrophe of discovery at the crossroads.
  - Dedicated standalone library reading page: `/library/?page=oedipus-the-king`.
- **Unit 3: Informational Analysis & Cultural Perspectives — *True Crime: The Roots of an American Obsession* (13 Lessons):**
  - Critical analysis of Walter Mosley’s provocative essay on human fascination with transgression and justice.
  - Evaluating central claims, structural development, scapegoating, moral clarity, and American individualism.
  - Dedicated standalone library reading page: `/library/?page=true-crime-walter-mosley`.

### Dedicated Library Reading Section Integration
Readings are integrated directly into the library section as standalone reading pages—not cataloged as books on shelves:
- **Dedicated Reading Endpoints:** Standalone URLs with numbered paragraphs and stanzas (`#p1`, `#p2`, etc.).
- **Reading Accessibility Tools:** Built-in Web Speech Synthesis read-aloud, font size adjustments, OpenDyslexic accessible font toggle, and 1-click clean printing.
- **Cross-Lesson Navigation:** Every lesson includes a prominent banner button to launch the full reading in the library in a new tab.

### Interactive Worksheet-First Experience
Every lesson provides an authentic, distraction-free worksheet layout:
- **Text-Dependent Analysis Problem Sets:** Questions extracted directly from EngageNY close-reading activities with auto-saving student textareas (`localStorage`).
- **Synthesizing Quick Writes:** Cumulative analytical writing prompts evaluated using the Short Response Rubric.
- **Teacher Solution Keys:** Collapsible solution accordions revealing worked analytical models, line citations, and exemplar student responses.
- **1-Click Browser Printing:** Supports printing blank worksheets or solved student homework packets.

### Accessibility & UDL Compliance
- **WCAG AA/AAA Color Independence:** Fully compliant contrast across Light, Midnight Dark, Sepia, and AAA High-Contrast presets.
- **Multimodal Design (UDL):** Visual text display, text-to-speech read-aloud, digital typing workspaces, and printable worksheets cater to diverse student learning modalities.
- **Offline Resiliency:** Fully cached by the PWA service worker; all 46 lessons and digital library readings remain accessible without an internet connection.
