## Grade 9 ELA Module 4: Sugar Changed the World & Historical Argument

### Executive Overview
Grade 9 ELA Module 4 delivers an intensive, 29-lesson investigation into world history, economic systems, and human rights through Marc Aronson and Marina Budhos' *Sugar Changed the World: A Story of Magic, Spice, Slavery, Freedom, and Science*, alongside investigative supplementary texts on modern globalization and the garment industry. Students trace how historical commodity trade catalyzed human migration and political revolutions, analyze contemporary labor ethics, and craft an argumentative research essay.

### Curriculum Structure & Unit Breakdown
* **Lessons 1–9: The Rise of Sugar & Global Commodities:** Analyzing "The Age of Honey," early oceanic trade, the plantation system, and how sugarcane transformed world diets and economics.
* **Lessons 10–14: Globalization & Modern Labor Ethics:** Evaluating investigative articles on fast fashion, the 2013 Rana Plaza factory collapse in Bangladesh, and global supply chains through `/library/?page=globalization`.
* **Lessons 15–24: The Haitian Revolution & Freedom Movements:** Exploring how sugar production directly spurred the fight for liberty in Saint-Domingue, examining the tension between economic profit and human rights.
* **Lessons 25–29: Argument Essay Drafting & Refinement:** Developing evidence-based claims and counterclaims, constructing parallel grammatical structures, establishing formal tone, and authoring a capstone argument essay.
