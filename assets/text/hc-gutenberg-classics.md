## Project Gutenberg Top Classics Digital Library & Unified Reader

Explore the world's most downloaded public-domain literary and historical masterworks directly on Hesten's Learning! Ingested from the official Project Gutenberg daily top downloads archive, all 31 celebrated volumes are fully integrated into our master catalog and unified digital reader.

### Quick Access & Discovery
- **Library Catalog Portal:** Visit `/library/index.php` or `/library/cataloge.php` to browse by subject desk (Classic Fiction, ELA, Civics, World History, General Resources).
- **Direct Digital Reading:** Click **Read Online** on any volume card or navigate directly via `/library/read/index.php?book=<book-id>`.
- **Gutenberg Numeric ID Jump:** Jump directly to any volume using its Gutenberg catalog number (e.g., `/library/read/index.php?book=11` for *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*, `/library/read/index.php?book=1342` for *Pride and Prejudice*, or `/library/read/index.php?book=84` for *Frankenstein*).

### Reading Suite Capabilities & Action Controls
- **Multi-Chapter Pagination:** Browse books chapter by chapter using the **Prev** and **Next** buttons in the sticky reading bar, or press `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight`.
- **Interactive Table of Contents (TOC):** Open the Table of Contents drawer modal from the sticky bar (`...` menu or TOC icon) to jump directly to any chapter with precomputed word counts and estimated reading minutes.
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS) Narration:** Listen aloud to any chapter with natural speech synthesis, adjustable narration speed (0.75x–2.0x), and play/pause controls (`Alt + P`).
- **Typography & Font Scaler:** Switch between Sans, Serif, OpenDyslexic, Atkinson Hyperlegible, and Monospace typography. Adjust font scale (`A-` / `100%` / `A+`) and line spacing (Tight, Normal, Relaxed, Loose).
- **Reading Themes:** Choose between Light, Sepia, Dark, and Midnight high-contrast themes.
- **Bionic Reading Fixations:** Toggle cognitive bionic reading mode to bold the initial letters of each word, guiding eye fixations and accelerating reading comprehension.
- **Distraction-Free Zen Mode:** Press `Z` or `Esc` to enter full-screen Zen mode, hiding all outer navigation bars and focusing purely on the text.
- **Dual-Page Spread Mode:** Toggle two-column book spread view on large desktop monitors for a classic hardcover reading experience.
- **Persistent Reading Progress:** The platform automatically records your chapter progress and scroll depth in local storage, providing a 1-click **Continue Reading** button when reopening any book.

### Accessibility & Universal Design for Learning (UDL)
- **100% Keyboard Operable:** Full navigation through `Tab`, `Shift+Tab`, `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight` (pages), `Space` (scroll), and `Esc` (close modals/zen).
- **High Contrast Compliant:** All text satisfies minimum 4.5:1 (AA) and 7:1 (AAA in Dark/Midnight modes) contrast ratios with explicit focus rings.
- **Multimodal Formats:** Free EPUB, PDF, and plain text download links are provided alongside inline browser reading for student device flexibility.
- **Offline Resiliency:** Books cached through the Service Worker can be read completely offline without an active internet connection.
