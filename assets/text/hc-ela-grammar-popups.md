## ELA Grammar Interactive Study Modals & Syntax Vaults

The **English Language Arts & Grammar Resource Terminal** provides students and educators with comprehensive, curriculum-aligned grammar lessons, mathematical syntax formulations, structural flowcharts, and formative practice checks across 6 fundamental language domains and 30 specialized topics:

1. **Parts of Speech**: Nouns & Pronouns, Verbs & Tenses, Adjectives & Adverbs, Prepositions & Conjunctions, Interjections & Articles
2. **Punctuation Rules**: Comma Usage, Semicolons & Colons, Apostrophes & Quotation Marks, Hyphens & Dashes, Parentheses & Ellipses
3. **Vocabulary Building**: Academic Word List, Prefixes & Suffixes, Context Clues, Synonym & Antonym Games, Roots & Etymology
4. **Common Errors Guide**: Homophones (their/there/they're), Run-on Sentences & Fragments, Subject-Verb Agreement Issues, Dangling Modifiers, Pronoun-Antecedent Agreement
5. **Sentence Structure**: Simple/Compound/Complex, Active vs. Passive Voice, Parallelism, Sentence Combining, Compound-Complex Sentences
6. **Figurative Language**: Metaphors & Similes, Personification & Hyperbole, Idioms & Allusions, Symbolism & Imagery, Alliteration & Onomatopoeia

### Core Features & Capabilities

- **Interactive Comprehensive Study Modals**: Clicking any grammar topic card or pill launches a dedicated learning module:
  - **Syntax Rules & Structural Formulations Vault**: Labeled syntactic formulas, morphological expansion laws, and concord rules typeset via crisp MathJax SVG notation.
  - **Visual Maps & Structural Diagrams**: Handcrafted vector diagrams (phonetic decision trees, pronoun agreement flowcharts, adjective hierarchy ladders, comma quadrant cards, morpheme assembly conveyors, and clause architecture blueprints).
  - **Curated Educational References & Academic Guides**: Direct links to Common Core Standards, Purdue OWL, Oxford Dictionaries, Cambridge Grammar, and Hesten's Learning Library.
  - **Concept Lecture & Subject Breakdown**: Rigorous academic definitions broken down into 3 structural pillars.
  - **Dual-Lexile Readability Toggle**: Instantly switch between *Standard Academic Lexile* and *Basic English (Simplified)* without losing context.
  - **Real-World Examples & Sentence Breakdowns**: Color-coded grammatical tags highlighting sentence constituents.
  - **Pro-Tips & Common Pitfalls**: Expert callouts addressing pervasive mistakes (such as the RAVEN rule for affect/effect and the Zombie test for passive voice).
  - **Formative Practice Checks**: Immediate self-assessment questions with instant validation and step-by-step explanatory breakdowns.
- **Universal MathJax SVG Typography**: All syntax rules and mathematical grammar formulations render with high contrast, crisp vector fidelity, and theme inheritance across light and dark modes.
- **Real-Time Topic Search & Domain Filters**: Filter grammar topics dynamically by typing keywords or clicking domain filter badges.

### Accessibility & Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

- **Multimodal Audio Read-Aloud**: Integrated Text-to-Speech (TTS) via the "Listen" button articulates definitions, examples, and rules.
- **Full Keyboard Navigation**: Complete modal focus management, `Escape` key dismissal, `Tab` cycling, and `Enter`/`Space` choice selection.
- **Dyslexia & Sensory Support**: Full compatibility with OpenDyslexic font toggling, Irlen color tints, bionic reading, and AAA high-contrast modes.
