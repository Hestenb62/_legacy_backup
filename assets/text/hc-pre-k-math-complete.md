## Pre-Kindergarten Mathematics Complete Curriculum & Interactive Worksheets

The **Pre-Kindergarten Mathematics Curriculum** (Level A) provides the foundational building blocks for early mathematical exploration, sensory discovery, and counting fluency aligned with EngageNY / Eureka Math early childhood guidelines. Through play-based and guided visual tasks, young children develop number sense, spatial awareness, and pattern recognition.

### Core Architecture & Coverage
Pre-Kindergarten features **146 sequential lessons** organized across 5 immersive modules:
- **Module 1: Counting to 5 (43 Lessons):** Matching and sorting objects by attribute, counting objects up to 5 in rows, arrays, and scattered configurations, one-to-one correspondence, and identifying numerals 1 through 5.
- **Module 2: Shapes (15 Lessons):** Exploring two-dimensional shapes (circles, squares, triangles, rectangles), shape attributes (round, straight, sides, corners), relative positions, and identifying shapes in the environment.
- **Module 3: Counting to 10 (40 Lessons):** Counting up to 10 objects, numeral recognition 6 through 10, one more/one less concept, ordering numerals 1 to 10, and tally marks.
- **Module 4: Comparison of Length, Weight, and Capacity (28 Lessons):** Comparing attributes directly (taller, shorter, heavier, lighter, holds more, holds less), balance scales, ordering sets of three objects by size, and early measurement reasoning.
- **Module 5: Addition and Subtraction Stories and Counting to 20 (20 Lessons):** Acting out addition and subtraction stories, putting together and taking apart small groups, counting forward to 20, and transitioning to Kindergarten math concepts.

### Interactive Worksheet-First Experience
Every lesson in Pre-K provides a clean, focused, distraction-free worksheet layout:
- **Direct Worksheet Display:** Pure focus on authentic problem set exercises, eliminating external links and research distractions.
- **Auto-Saving Digital Scratchpads:** Interactive textareas for every problem that persist student work in `localStorage` and synchronize with the cloud.
- **Teacher Solution Keys:** Instant expandable solution accordions displaying step-by-step mathematical reasoning and answers.
- **1-Click Browser Printing:**
  - **Print Blank:** Outputs cleanly formatted, paper-based worksheets with student name lines and ruled handwriting boxes.
  - **Print Solved Work:** Formats typed student notes and solutions into a completed homework packet ready for submission.

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Action Controls
- `Tab` / `Shift + Tab`: Seamlessly navigate through problem cards, action buttons, and digital scratchpad fields.
- `Space` / `Enter`: Expand and collapse teacher solution accordions and toggle print modes.
- `Ctrl + P` (or `Cmd + P`): Direct trigger for high-fidelity print output.
- `Esc`: Dismiss modal overlays and exit ticket assessment runners.

### Accessibility & UDL Compliance
- **Universal MathJax SVG Rendering:** Every mathematical formula and symbol scales dynamically, supports screen magnification up to 400%, and inherits high-contrast foreground color (`currentColor`).
- **WCAG AA/AAA Color Independence:** Meets strict contrast requirements in Light, Midnight Dark, Sepia, and AAA High-Contrast presets.
- **Assistive Technology Optimization:** Every problem card, solution accordion, and input area is equipped with semantic ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label`, `aria-controls`).
- **Offline Resiliency:** Fully cached by the PWA service worker; all 5 modules and interactive practice worksheets remain completely accessible without an internet connection.
