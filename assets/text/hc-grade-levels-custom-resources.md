## Grade-Level Curriculum Pages & Custom Educational Resources

The Grade-Level Curriculum Pages provide a complete, developmentally sequenced learning path spanning Pre-K through 12th Grade and Advanced Placement preparation. Each level page features standards-aligned modules across Mathematics, English Language Arts, Science Inquiry, and Social Studies, paired with age-appropriate digital library literature, interactive virtual manipulatives, and pedagogical study strategies.

### Target Audience & Developmental Brackets
- **Early Learners (Pre-K – Kindergarten, Levels A & B)**: Foundations in counting, shapes, phonemic awareness, and story exploration.
- **Primary & Elementary (1st – 5th Grade, Levels C – G)**: Operations within 20 and 100, place value regrouping, multiplication arrays, fraction models, and reading fluency.
- **Middle School (6th – 8th Grade, Levels H – J)**: Ratios, unit rates, proportional relationships, negative integers, linear equations, and foundational civics.
- **High School (9th – 12th Grade, Levels K – N)**: Algebra I, coordinate geometry, proofs, polynomial functions, pre-calculus, calculus, and rhetorical analysis.
- **AP & College Prep (Level O)**: Chronological historical analysis, Document-Based Questions (DBQs), and full Practice GED high school equivalency testing.

### Core Capabilities & Navigation
- **Dynamic Subject Breadcrumbs**: Every grade page includes an accessible breadcrumb trail (`Home / Levels / Level [X] ([Grade]) / [Subject]`) that updates in real time when toggling between Mathematics, Language Arts, Science, and Social Studies.
- **Custom Curriculum Literature**: The sidebar highlights classic literature and primary texts drawn from the platform's open-access reading library, matching the reading level and historical themes of each specific grade.
- **Tailored Interactive Tools**: Direct access to grade-specific learning workbenches—including virtual ten-frames, clocks, geoboards, fraction strips, middle school ratio workbenches, and quadratic graphing utilities.
- **Pedagogical Study Strategies**: Actionable, grade-targeted study tips guiding learners on tactile counting, friendly number bonds, fraction benchmark models, unit rate calculations, equation balance scales, and document corroboration.
- **Lesson Check & Bookmarking**: Mark skills as mastered with celebratory visual feedback, bookmark standards for review, or launch standard-targeted diagnostic assessments with one click.

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Action Controls
- `Tab` / `Shift+Tab`: Navigate sequentially through breadcrumbs, subject tabs, live skill search, and resource cards.
- `Enter` or `Space`: Activate subject tabs (Math, ELA, Science, Social Studies), expand lesson cards, or launch interactive workbench tools.
- `Alt + 1` / `Alt + 2` / `Alt + 3` / `Alt + 4`: Quick jump between core academic subjects.
- `Esc`: Dismiss active standard highlight banners or search filters.

### Accessibility & Universal Design for Learning (UDL)
- **Screen Reader Support**: All breadcrumb items, hero badges, literature links, and interactive tools feature descriptive semantic labels and ARIA attributes (`aria-current="page"`, `aria-selected`, `aria-label`).
- **High Contrast & Theme Adaptability**: Cards, badges, and focus rings meet or exceed WCAG 2.1/2.2 AAA contrast standards (7:1 in high-contrast mode) across light, dark, and sensory retreat modes.
- **Multimodal Representation**: Supports read-aloud text-to-speech, OpenDyslexic font toggling, Irlen color overlays, and responsive typography scaling down to 320px mobile viewports.
- **Offline Resiliency**: All curriculum outline data, reading texts, and practice workbenches are cached for offline access through the platform service worker.
