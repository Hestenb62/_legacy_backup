## Grade 1 Mathematics Complete Curriculum & Interactive Worksheets

The **Grade 1 Mathematics Curriculum** (Level C) develops early numeracy, structural addition and subtraction concepts, and spatial reasoning aligned with EngageNY / Eureka Math and national Common Core standards. It guides young learners from counting on and making ten toward place value understanding and geometric problem solving.

### Core Architecture & Coverage
Grade 1 features **153 sequential lessons** organized across 6 foundational modules:
- **Module 1: Sums and Differences to 10 (39 Lessons):** Embedded numbers and decompositions of 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10, counting on, addition word problems (result unknown, change unknown), subtraction as an unknown addend, and subtraction word problems.
- **Module 2: Introduction to Place Value Through Addition and Subtraction Within 20 (29 Lessons):** Counting on and making ten, take-from-ten strategies, teen numbers as ten and ones, and word problems with up to three addends.
- **Module 3: Ordering and Comparing Length Measurements as Numbers (13 Lessons):** Indirect comparison of lengths, non-standard units of measurement (paper clips, cubes), standard length measurement, and data representation with tally charts and picture graphs.
- **Module 4: Place Value, Comparison, Addition and Subtraction to 40 (29 Lessons):** Tens and ones place value, comparing two-digit numbers using $<$, $>$, and $=$, adding tens to two-digit numbers, adding a pair of two-digit numbers with decomposing/composing a ten.
- **Module 5: Identifying, Composing, and Partitioning Shapes (13 Lessons):** Attributes of 2D and 3D shapes (cylinders, spheres, prisms, cones, rectangles, squares, circles, triangles), composing composite shapes, and partitioning halves and quarters of circles and rectangles.
- **Module 6: Place Value, Comparison, Addition and Subtraction to 100 (30 Lessons):** Counting and writing numbers to 120, place value to 100, addition and subtraction strategies within 100, dimes and pennies, and celebration of mathematical learning.

### Interactive Worksheet-First Experience
Every lesson in Grade 1 provides a clean, focused, distraction-free worksheet layout:
- **Direct Worksheet Display:** Pure focus on authentic problem set exercises, eliminating external links and research distractions.
- **Auto-Saving Digital Scratchpads:** Interactive textareas for every problem that persist student work in `localStorage` and synchronize with the cloud.
- **Teacher Solution Keys:** Instant expandable solution accordions displaying step-by-step mathematical reasoning and answers.
- **1-Click Browser Printing:**
  - **Print Blank:** Outputs cleanly formatted, paper-based worksheets with student name lines and ruled handwriting boxes.
  - **Print Solved Work:** Formats typed student notes and solutions into a completed homework packet ready for submission.

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Action Controls
- `Tab` / `Shift + Tab`: Seamlessly navigate through problem cards, action buttons, and digital scratchpad fields.
- `Space` / `Enter`: Expand and collapse teacher solution accordions and toggle print modes.
- `Ctrl + P` (or `Cmd + P`): Direct trigger for high-fidelity print output.
- `Esc`: Dismiss modal overlays and exit ticket assessment runners.

### Accessibility & UDL Compliance
- **Universal MathJax SVG Rendering:** Every mathematical formula and symbol scales dynamically, supports screen magnification up to 400%, and inherits high-contrast foreground color (`currentColor`).
- **WCAG AA/AAA Color Independence:** Meets strict contrast requirements in Light, Midnight Dark, Sepia, and AAA High-Contrast presets.
- **Assistive Technology Optimization:** Every problem card, solution accordion, and input area is equipped with semantic ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label`, `aria-controls`).
- **Offline Resiliency:** Fully cached by the PWA service worker; all 6 modules and interactive practice worksheets remain completely accessible without an internet connection.
