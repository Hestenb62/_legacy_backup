## Unified Scratchpad & Math Whiteboard Studio

Empower your study sessions with the Unified Scratchpad & Math Whiteboard Studio! Whether drafting analytical essays, solving multi-step algebra derivations, or sketching scientific diagrams, the scratchpad combines tabbed digital notebooks with a responsive mathematical whiteboard canvas.

### Quick Access & Keyboard Shortcuts
You can launch the studio from anywhere on Hesten's Learning without losing your place:
- **Keyboard Shortcut:** Press `Alt + S` on any page to open or close the scratchpad.
- **Floating Action Button (FAB):** Click the pencil icon located in the bottom-right action launcher.
- **Learning Launchpad:** Click the "Scratchpad" quick-action card on the home page.

### Core Superpowers & Tabs

- **1. Multi-Page Notebooks:** Create, switch between, and rename multiple notebook pages (`Alt + N` for new note). Every note autosaves locally to browser storage with cross-tab synchronization.
- **2. Academic Study Templates:** One-click loading of Cornell Notes, K-W-L inquiry charts, Study Guides, Lecture Notes, MLA, APA, Chicago, and Harvard essay formatting templates.
- **3. Interactive Drawing Whiteboard:** Switch to the Whiteboard tab to sketch freehand diagrams with customizable drawing pens, semi-transparent highlighters, geometric shapes (lines, arrows, rectangles, circles), and 30-step undo/redo (`Ctrl + Z` / `Ctrl + Y`).
- **4. Math Grid Paper:** Toggle between Blank, Dot Grid, Cartesian Coordinate Graph Paper, and Isometric 3D grids for geometric constructions.
- **5. Formula Quick-Insert & Live MathJax:** Click chips in the math palette to insert algebraic fractions, exponents, square roots, integrals, summations, and Greek symbols ($E=mc^2$). The live preview renders beautiful, responsive MathJax SVG equations automatically.
- **6. Side-by-Side Docked Split-View:** Click the column dock icon (`#scratchpad-dock-btn`) in the header to pin the scratchpad to the right margin of your screen. Read lessons, stories, or complete quizzes on the left while simultaneously taking notes on the right!
- **7. Assistive Voice Dictation:** Click "Dictate" (`Alt + D`) to transcribe spoken notes with real-time Speech-to-Text.
- **8. Rich Exporting:** One-click "Copy", download as Plain Text (`.txt`), formatted Markdown (`.md`), or export canvas sketches as high-resolution PNG snapshots.

### Universal Accessibility (UDL)
The studio includes custom font family toggles (OpenDyslexic, Lexend, Monospace), font size scaling (A- / A+), full screen reader ARIA live region support, and works 100% offline via local Progressive Web App caching.
